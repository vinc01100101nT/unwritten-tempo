# Cards

*Monster-matchup specialization. **(Planned feature — not yet in the game.)***

## What cards will be

Cards are a Ragnarok-style progression axis, separate from your Ring and your gear stats. A card slots into a piece of equipment and grants **bonuses or resistances against specific enemy categories or damage types** — for example, extra damage to a monster family, or resistance to an element.

The guiding rules for the design:

- **Cards only affect combat outcomes against tagged enemies** — they never change your skills.
- They're meant to be **matchup flavor, not raw power** — a way to specialize for what you're fighting, not a second power-stacking track.
- Slot count is loosely tied to gear **rarity** (see *Weapons & Equipment*).

## Status

Cards are **deferred**. They depend on a monster-category/tagging system that hasn't been built yet, and they'll arrive together with it. This page will fill in with the real card list once the feature lands.
