# Getting Started

*Your first steps as a husk. (This page grows as the early game is finalized.)*

## You begin as a husk

You wake with no color — one of the many who lost their identity when the world's truth was un-written. Your goal from the first minute is the goal of the whole game: **hunt the scattered pages** (the monsters), recover the truth, and win your color back.

## The loop, in one breath

1. **Fight monsters** — each one is a corrupted page. Defeating it recovers a fragment.
2. **Level up** — every level lets you draft a point of growth on your **Ring** (see *Stats & the Ring*).
3. **Equip a weapon** — your weapon decides which **skills** you can use. Swap weapons to swap your kit.
4. **Grow into a role** — where you invest your ring points quietly commits you to a role (a bruiser, a mage, a healer…).
5. **Get your color back** — as you progress, identity leaks back into your husk.

## What decides your power

- **Your Ring** — your stat growth and the heart of your build. See **Stats & the Ring**.
- **Your weapon** — grants your active skills. See **Skills** and **Weapons & Equipment**.
- **Your gear & cards** — armor, accessories, and (planned) cards that specialize you. See **Weapons & Equipment** and **Cards**.
