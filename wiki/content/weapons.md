# Weapons & Equipment

*Gear that shapes your kit, your defense, and your look.*

## Weapons decide your skills

Your weapon is more than a stat stick — it **grants the active skills** on your hotbar (see *Skills*). Every weapon is a real 3D object your character holds, and swapping weapons swaps your whole kit.

### Weapon types

| Type                   | Feel                                     |
| ---------------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| **Sword**              | Balanced one-hander; pairs with a shield |
| **Two-Hand Sword**     | Heavy hitter                             |
| **Dagger**             | Fast, low per-hit; strong vs small foes  |
| **Axe / Two-Hand Axe** | Big, slow, heavy damage                  |
| **Mace**               | Blunt / holy flavor                      |
| **Spear**              | Reach                                    |
| **Staff**              | The magic weapon (drives Magic Attack)   |
| **Bow**                | Physical ranged, kiting                  |
| **Katar**              | Assassin double-dagger, crit-leaning     |
| **Knuckle**            | Monk / fist                              |
| **Firearm**            | Ranged firearms                          |

## Equipment slots

Beyond your weapon, you wear:

- **Head**, **Armor**, **Garment**, **Shield**, and two **Accessory** slots
- Your **Ring** — the vessel of your growth (see *Stats & the Ring*)

Each piece adds stats and defense; some carry set flavor or elemental properties.

## Refinement (+N)

Gear can be **refined** to higher plus-levels:

- A **weapon** gains **+3 Attack per refine level**.
- **Every** piece of gear gains **+1 Defense per refine level**.

So a +10 weapon is +30 Attack over its base, and any +10 piece is +10 Defense.

## Rarity

Gear comes in rarity tiers — **Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary** — which drive its border color and, loosely, how many **card slots** it has.

## Cards

Higher-rarity gear has **card slots** you can socket with cards for monster-matchup bonuses. See **Cards**.
