# The Sword

*A duelist's weapon — precise single-target bursts and timed defense. You win fights one opponent at a time, by reading the fight and striking at the right moment.*

> **Status:** design locked, numbers in tuning. The Sword is the first fully-designed weapon kit — one active skill is still being finalized.

## The fantasy

The Sword isn't about crowds or flashy area attacks — it's about **the duel**. You stay in your opponent's face, build rhythm as you trade blows, and turn that rhythm into one decisive moment. Its precision comes from **timing**, not luck: there are no critical-hit tricks here, just reading the fight and hitting the beat.

## How a Sword's kit works

Like every weapon, the Sword draws from a **pool of skills** — an **ultimate**, several **active** skills, and several **passives** — and each Sword that drops rolls its own kit from that pool. Higher-rarity Swords arrive more complete and are likelier to carry an ultimate; you craft the rest at the forge. Each ultimate has **partner skills** built to amplify it — roll an ultimate *and* its partners and you've got that build at its best.

## Ultimates — three ways to win

Every Sword can carry **one**.

- **Deathblow** — *the finisher.* Pour everything into one enormous strike; the more **Momentum** you've built, the more devastating it lands. *(Partner: the Momentum & Perfect Form passives.)*
- **Bladedance** — *the storm.* Become a whirlwind on one target, **completely untouchable** while it lasts. Faster attacks = more strikes. *(Partners: Overclock, Encore.)*
- **Retribution** — *the counter.* Brace and soak everything, then hurl it all back in one uncapped blow. The bigger the hits you weather, the bigger the payback. *(Partners: Parry, Last Stand.)*

## Active skills

The skills you press.

- **Lunge** — dash to a target and strike; your way to open the duel.
- **Parry** — block an incoming hit completely and sharpen your next strike. Shines during **Retribution**: parry mid-brace to survive *and* still bank the blow, and chain-parry a flurry to stack the payback.
- **Guard Stance** — take much less damage for a few seconds; a dependable panic button.
- **Overclock** — swing much faster for a while, at the cost of taking double damage briefly. That drawback flips per ultimate — fuel for Deathblow, *more* payback for Retribution, and pure upside under Bladedance's invulnerability.
- **Feint** — a quick jab that punishes an enemy caught mid-swing; reward for reading their tell.
- **Committed Strike** — wind up and commit to one heavy, telegraphed blow.
- **Disarm** — a strike that briefly blunts the enemy's next attack; good when your counter's on cooldown.
- **Onslaught** — unleash a rapid flurry of ordinary strikes, building Momentum fast.
- *(One more active is still being designed.)*

## Passive skills

Traits you carry — always working, no button to press.

- **Momentum** — every basic attack builds a stack; the more you stack, the harder you hit (and the bigger your Deathblow). Attack speed keeps the stacks alive. *(Deathblow's partner.)*
- **Perfect Form** — staying locked on one target sharpens your rhythm: more Momentum, slower to fade. Break off and it resets.
- **Encore** — a kill during **Bladedance** extends the storm.
- **Last Stand** — near death, your **Retribution** counter can't be interrupted and its parry-chain holds firm — the desperate comeback.
- **Composure** — after a clean Parry, your next skill costs nothing.
- **Unwavering** — while braced behind Guard Stance, nothing can stagger or shove you.
- **Opening** — your first strike on a fresh, full-health foe reveals their next attack — set the pace of the duel.
- **Pressure** — relentless hits on the same target keep it from recovering health.
- **Lock Blades** — after you Lunge, your quarry can't slip away — you stay glued to the duel.

*Not every skill a weapon can roll is a superstar — part of the fun is discovering which fit your build. The Sword rewards players who commit to the fight and learn its rhythm.*
