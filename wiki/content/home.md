# Unwritten Wiki

*The official guide to **Unwritten** — a 2.5D MMORPG where you hunt the scattered pages of a broken world to win back your own color.*

> **Status:** Living wiki, in active development. Pages reflect the current design; anything marked *planned* or *in tuning* isn't final yet. Numbers and rosters are still being balanced before launch.

## What is Unwritten?

Long ago, someone wrote down the **truth of the world** in a single book. A hostile force **un-wrote it** — tearing the record apart. The torn pages scattered across the land and, where they fell, twisted into **monsters**. With the truth gone, people lost their identities: their color drained away, leaving them as **charcoal husks**.

You are one of those husks. You wander an endless world, hunting the lost pages, reassembling the truth — and as you recover it, your **color returns**. That gradual return of self *is* your progression.

The look of the game is the story: the flat, papery monsters **are** the corrupted pages; the 3D-but-colorless heroes **are** people who lost their ink.
