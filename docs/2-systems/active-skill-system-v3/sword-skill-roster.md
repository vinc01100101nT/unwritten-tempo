# Sword — Skill Roster (V3)

The first fully-authored weapon. Companion to **Weapon Roster — 14 Identities** (identity litmus), **Active Skill System — V3** (channels/effects/scaling), **Skill Numbers & Balance Baseline** (bands), and **State System** (buffs/debuffs/marks). Converged over a long ult-first design pass + a GPT stress-test round.

**Identity:** *Win duels through precise single-target bursts and timed defense.* Might / Atk · Burst. Everything is **single-target**.

**Hard bans (identity walls):** no **crit**, no **armor-pierce** (crit is Finesse's identity — and in our engine crit is the *only* thing that ignores DEF, so giving Sword crit hands it Finesse's whole mechanic), no **AoE / DoT / heal / execute**. **Precision = TIMING (Bending Reed), never crit.**

## Design guide — the universal-but-specialized rule (heuristic, not law)

**Every ult should have at least one normal that *specializes* in it while still benefiting the other two ults.** This builds a shared spine so the three ult-builds aren't siloed mini-kits. Rules:

- The specialization must be **asymmetric and real** — one ult clearly gains the most, or the skill is filler.
- Cap it at **\~1 per ult**; keep the *other* normals **sharply single-purpose** (else the roster blurs into "lean-buffs").
- It's a **heuristic, not a law** — don't force the resource→specialist→ult shape onto weapons whose identity doesn't want it (e.g. Sniper may need only one setup skill).
- **Not every normal must be build-optimal.** Some weaker "trash-ish" slots are intentional — players discover which skills suit their build. A perfectly flat roster where every skill is equally good is *not* the goal.

Sword's spine: **Rising Breeze → Last Petal · Held Breath → Returning Wind · Squall → Thousand Petals.** All three payoffs are authored `tier = Ultimate` in the assets.

## Rising Breeze — Last Petal's partner (a passive skill you buy, NOT default)

**Rising Breeze is one of the Sword's passive-pool skills — a skill you *buy* with Sword mastery points, not intrinsic weapon behavior.** It's Last Petal's partner in the spine (a partner may be an **active or a passive** — Bending Reed/Squall are active partners, Rising Breeze is the passive one). A swordsman only has Rising Breeze if they bought it.

- **Consequence:** Last Petal spends Rising Breeze's pile, so it **requires** Rising Breeze — every Last Petal build has it. With no stacks up, Last Petal is a flat **\~600% ATK** nuke with **no** +40%/stack scaling; it stays usable, it just doesn't scale.

- **Generated by successful basic attacks** (+1 stack each). **Not** from any active skill — this is why Attack Speed matters.

- V3 State: **Private, Stacking**, each stack its own duration, **FIFO** expiry, **no refresh exceptions**. **As composed: 5s per stack, ceiling 99** — high enough that the equilibrium below, not the ceiling, is what bounds it.

- Each stack = **+3% damage** (as composed) applied to **all** outgoing damage — basics, actives, all three ults.

- **Equilibrium, not infinity:** because stacks expire on FIFO timers, your stable count ≈ **attack rate × stack duration**. Attack Speed is the lever; the new **Attack Speed cap** (below) bounds the ceiling so it can't run away.

- **Last Petal double-dips Rising Breeze by design** (its specialty) — see below.

- **State-order (LOCKED, closes the S2 ambiguity):** on Last Petal cast → **snapshot stack count → compute *both* Last Petal's +40%/stack scaling AND the standing attack% off that snapshot → deal damage → THEN consume all stacks.** Never consume before reading.

## Ultimates — THREE, as composed (re-confirmed 2026-08-24 off the assets)

All three are authored `tier = Ultimate` and priced as ultimates (**40 MP**, long cooldown, no charge meter — see **Skill Numbers & Balance Baseline**, cost & cooldown rules). Casting any of them also starts the **40 s shared cooldown** on every ultimate, on top of each one's own.

| Ult | Payoff | Effect | Numbers (as composed) | Specialist feeder |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Last Petal** | Execution spike | One massive hit; **consumes all Rising Breeze** | **600% ATK + 40%/stack** **×** the standing Rising Breeze damage% (deliberate double-dip) · 40 MP · 60s cd · 1s cast · range 5 | **Rising Breeze** |
| **Returning Wind** | Reactive counter | Cash the damage **Held Breath** delayed, and **the debt is erased** — you never pay what you release | **5 strikes, each 100% ATK + 250% of the banked total** (not a split — every strike lands the whole amount) · **NO CAP** · plain strike (no reflect recursion) · **fires on an empty bank too**, for five plain attacks · 40 MP · 45s cd · range 11 | **Held Breath** |
| **Thousand Petals** | Sustained storm | Two attack-speed-sized piles, then a beat that walks target to target | Dance charges = `ceil(8 ÷ seconds-per-swing)`; Parry charges = half that (`stackShare 0.5`); both **8s**. **3 beats per swing**, each spends one dance charge, hops a **7-corner star** with a **1s afterimage**, strikes **100% ATK**, re-aims within **3 units**. Each held dance charge = **+1% damage** · 40 MP · 45s cd · range 11 | **Squall** |

**The structural question is answered by the data:** Sword ships with three ultimates. Nothing else in this doc assumed that, so treat any "one ult per weapon" line elsewhere as the older assumption.

## What is actually composed — read it, don't restate it

**`data/skills.md` is the generated table of every composed skill**: its two-letter **symbol**, tier, cost, trigger and
what it does, grouped by weapon, with each weapon's **colour** named as a hex code. It is written by the Skill
Composer's Save from the assets themselves, so it can never disagree with the game.

**The Sword's colour is `#FF0F00`.** Its symbols, in the composer's order: `Bd` Thousand Petals · `Br` Held Breath ·
`Ch` Howling Wind · `Cs` Single Petal · `Cp` Eye of the Storm · `Db` Last Petal · `Ds` Stripped Bough · `En` Second Bloom · `Fl` Tailwind ·
`Gs` Still Air · `Ls` Bare Branch · `Lb` Crossed Branches · `Lu` Piercing Gust · `Mo` Rising Breeze · `Ob` Scattered Petals · `On` Petal Storm ·
`Op` Parting Clouds · `Oc` Squall · `Pa` Bending Reed · `Pr` Headwind · `Rt` Returning Wind · `Un` Deep Roots.

*A symbol is two letters standing for a skill the way `Au` stands for gold — what a tile shows when there is no room
for a name. It is authored on the skill; the colour is authored on the WEAPON, so every Sword skill wears the same
one. This doc states them for a reader's convenience; the generated table is the authority.*

**This doc owns INTENT — why a skill exists, what it must never do, what is still undecided.** Numbers stated below
are for that argument's sake; when one disagrees with the generated table, the table is right and this line is stale.

## Pool status

**22 sword skills are composed** (read off the assets 2026-08-24): 3 ultimates, 10 actives, 9 passives. **All 22 are** `published = 1` (publishing opens a skill to the forge's trademark roll) — Headwind was the last holdout and was published on 2026-08-24.

**Composed 2026-08-24 — the last five, so nothing in this roster is design-only any more:** **Stripped Bough**, **Howling Wind** and **Scattered Petals** (actives), **Bare Branch** and **Eye of the Storm** (passives). Howling Wind and Scattered Petals fill the two open active slots (the unresolved one and the one Feint vacated). Bare Branch and Eye of the Storm were both re-shaped to reach something the engine actually has — see their blocks below.

**Perfect Form is gone — it is now** `skill.sword.flow`**, "Tailwind".** Same job, reshaped onto the engine's automatic run-of-hits counter instead of a hand-rolled same-target streak. Do not re-add Perfect Form.

**No slots remain open.** The last two — the unresolved single-purpose texture slot and the one Feint vacated when it was cut on 2026-08-18 for reading as Bending Reed-with-damage — were filled by Howling Wind and Scattered Petals. The pool has had its **Stage-7 stress-test**; findings are folded into the implementation items below (per-hit state order, OnBasicAttack recursion guard, Lock-Blades pursue definition). The player-facing **wiki** (`wiki/content/sword.json`) reflects the current pool.

## Active skills (10 composed, none open)

Two are **spine partners** (carry an ult); the rest are single-purpose texture.

**Held Breath** *(Returning Wind's spine partner)* — *Take it now, pay it later — or don't pay at all.* Arms **5 charges for 4s**. Each charge holds back ONE incoming hit: instead of costing HP, that hit's damage is **banked**, and a cold-blue number pops for it. Out of charges, the next hit lands normally. **12 MP · 7s cd.**

- **The bill comes due when the window ends** — everything banked lands at once as a single **true-damage** hit (its own purple number). Held Breath DELAYS damage, it never prevents it.
- **What's banked is what would really have cost HP:** the damage-reduction pile (in either direction) and Shield all apply *before* the hit is recorded, and a parried hit never lands at all, so it banks nothing. Poison/bleed/burn ticks bypass it entirely.
- **Its only escape is Returning Wind** (below). Cashing the pile erases the debt — that IS Returning Wind's perk. With Returning Wind still cooling down — on its own cooldown or the shared cooldown — bracing is a bet you will lose.
- **The cooldown deliberately exceeds the window** (7s vs 4s), so a second cast can't chain-postpone the bill forever.
- *Engine note: two states — the armed charges (spend down, can hit zero) and the debt (must outlive them). Bending Reed's exact shape, one word apart: a parry **erases** the hit, a brace **postpones** it.*

**Bending Reed** — *Read the hit and punish it.* Blocks the next incoming hit fully (**0 HP**) within a **3s** window; **1 charge**, spent by the hit it erases. **8 MP · 5s cd** (the cooldown deliberately exceeds the window, so parry can't be held up permanently).

- **As composed, Bending Reed grants no damage bonus of its own.** The old "next-strike +50%" is not in the asset; the parry payoff now lives entirely in the **Parting Clouds** passive, so the two can't stack.
- ⚠️ **STALE — needs a design decision (2026-08-16).** Everything below was written when Returning Wind owned its own brace and Bending Reed was its spine partner. **Held Breath is now a separate skill and Returning Wind's partner**, and the two do not interact: a parried hit is erased before any damage number exists, so it **banks nothing**. The auto-parry chain, the release bonus and the compounding tower described below are all unimplemented and currently unreachable. **Decide whether Bending Reed gets a new role or keeps standing alone, then rewrite this block.**

**Squall** (was "Flurry") *(Thousand Petals' spine partner)* — *Go faster, fragile.* **+30% Attack Speed for 8s, but take 2× damage for 3s.** The 2× drawback **flips per build**:

- **Thousand Petals** (specialist): attack speed buys **more charges AND more parries** (both counts are derived from your swing rate), and it beats faster. ⚠️ **The old "its full invuln suppresses the 2×" no longer holds** — Thousand Petals grants no invulnerability, so the 2× is a real cost you take with parries as the only cover. Reads as a bigger, more honest gamble than it used to.
- **Returning Wind:** 2× incoming → **2× bank** — drawback becomes upside.
- **Last Petal:** aspd → faster basics → Rising Breeze quicker, but fragile for 3s.
- 12 MP · 12s cd. *(Watch-item: trends universally desirable; the 2× cost is the governor for every build now.)*

**Thousand Petals** *(Squall's specialist — BUILT 2026-08-16, and an **ULTIMATE** as composed — its full row is in the Ultimates table above)* — *Become the storm.* **40 MP · 45s cd · 11 range.** On cast it hands you **two piles that both last 8s and are both sized by your swing rate** — dance charges (`ceil(8 ÷ your seconds-per-swing)`) and **half that many, rounded up, in Parry charges**. Then it beats: **3 beats per swing**, each beat **spends one dance charge**, hops to the next of **7 star corners** leaving a **1s afterimage**, and strikes for **100% ATK**. Each beat re-aims at a random living mob within **3 units of the mob it just struck**, so the dance walks target to target; nothing in range ends it. **One beat, one target — this is single-target, never AoE.**

- Each held dance charge is also **+1% damage** (they are a stacking Damage buff), so the storm is strongest at its start and tapers as it spends down.
- **Defence is the Parry pile, not invulnerability** — it erases hits until it runs out, then you are exposed. Deliberate: a defence you can drain is a decision, not a freebie.
- **Measured consequence of the current tuning:** at base speed that is **14 slashes over \~2.8s** (14 × 0.2s) while the **parries last the full 8s** — so the guard outlives the dance by \~5s. Set beats-per-swing to 1 if you want the dance to fill its own window instead.
- *Engine note: no code names this skill. It composes from* `repeatCostsCharge` *+* `repeatScaleWithAttackSpeed` *+* `repeatReaim` *+ attack-speed-derived stack counts + the authored star corner count. See the implementation notes.*

**Piercing Gust** — *Close the gap and strike.* Dash to a target + strike. The "reach" glue. 100% ATK · 8 MP · 5s cd · reach **8 world units** (`castRange`; the unit every range in this doc is written in — not navigation cells, which are 0.5 each). **Combo:** if you also bought **Crossed Branches** (which requires Piercing Gust), casting Piercing Gust grants it **5 charges** (tunable) — a Piercing Gust+Crossed Branches build keeps a near-constant supply of auto-lunges.

**Still Air** — *Weather it.* Take **50% less damage for 6s**. 12 MP · 9s cd. *(It was briefly authored backwards — a* `SubStatDebuff` *on* `DamageReduction`*, which the applier negates, so it INCREASED damage taken. Fixed to* `SubStatBuff` *on 2026-08-24 and verified off the asset. Squall still uses the debuff kind deliberately — that is how it doubles incoming damage.)* ⚠️ **The asset's own description string still says "for 5 seconds" while the effect runs 6s** — one or the other should move. **Intentionally the weakest slot** (dominated by Bending Reed/Thousand Petals, anti-synergizes Returning Wind) — kept as the always-available sustain floor and a deliberate "not every skill is optimal" slot.

**~~Feint~~ — CUT 2026-08-18.** It read as Bending Reed with damage bolted on: both were "read the incoming attack and answer it", and the roster only needs one such verb. Its payoff partner **Parting Clouds now rides Bending Reed directly** (see passives), which also retires the mob-telegraph work Feint alone required. The slot it vacated is **open** — see the open-slot note below.

**Single Petal** — *Wind up and commit.* A slow, telegraphed **heavy single hit** — the pool's one true **damage-typed** skill (Physical `damageMult`). **200% ATK · 16 MP · 8s cd · 1.2s cast · range 5.** Pairs with **Deep Roots** (guard up so nothing shoves you out of the wind-up).

**Stripped Bough** — **COMPOSED 2026-08-24.** *Blunt their swing.* A strike for **100% ATK** that hands the target **one charge that makes its next attack land 40% weaker**, held for 6s. **12 MP · 9s cd · range 5.** It weakens, it does **not** deny/stun — stays clear of Warhammer. Anti-burst tool when Returning Wind is on cooldown. *Engine note: the charge is the exact mirror of Parting Clouds's boost, spent by the attack it blunts whether that attack hits or misses.*

**Howling Wind** — **COMPOSED 2026-08-24.** *Make them fight you.* Taunts ONE enemy for **5s**: locked onto you, **cannot use skills**, and **no pause between swings** — its swing rate is untouched, the gaps are. **12 MP · 8s cd · range 11 · no damage** (the lock is the whole skill). Its point is Held Breath and Bare Branch: a taunted target cannot decline to feed the bank. *Engine note: the taunt's rules now live on the taunt itself, so they hold for whoever it lands on — see the implementation items.*

**Scattered Petals** — **COMPOSED 2026-08-24.** *Break their rhythm.* A strike for **100% ATK** that cuts the target's **Attack Speed by 30% for 5s**. **12 MP · 8s cd · range 5.** Fewer swings means every Bending Reed and Held Breath window covers more of the fight — timed defence bought from the offensive side. Not a Slow and not a stun: nothing is denied, only made rarer.

**Petal Storm** — *Unleash a flurry.* An instant burst of **real basic attacks** (no skill `damageMult`; ATK-based) — so it feeds **Rising Breeze** and rides every OnBasicAttack passive. **12 MP · 8s cd · range 11.** As composed it arms **5 stacks of +1% damage for 5s (ceiling 7)** and then spends them as the flurry's charges: **2 beats per swing**, one stack per beat, so the stacks ARE the shot count and the damage bonus tapers as it fires. Distinct from Squall (a sustained *buff*); Petal Storm is an on-demand Rising Breeze dump.

**No active slots are open any more** — Howling Wind and Scattered Petals took the last two on 2026-08-24.

## Passive skills (9 composed)

Behavioral, event-triggered (via the trigger system Rising Breeze proves). **Each ult has a special passive partner** — some universal-but-specialized, some ult-exclusive (fine: passives are bought per build, so an exclusive one is build-defining, not dead). **Every one costs 0 MP and has no cooldown** — the pricing rule lives in **Skill Numbers & Balance Baseline**.

- **Rising Breeze** *(Last Petal partner)* — see its own section above. `OnBasicAttackDealt` → **+1 stack, +3% damage each, 5s per stack, ceiling 99**; Last Petal consumes the pile.
- **Tailwind** *(Last Petal partner — replaces the old "Perfect Form")* — `OnBasicAttackDealt`, gated on the engine's **automatic run of hits** (`@run-of-hits`, the unit's unbroken run against ONE body): each basic attack grants **extra Rising Breeze equal to ⌊run × 0.33⌋** — so about **+1 more per hit from the third hit of a run, +2 from the ninth**. The run is ended by the fight (a switched target, or a hit that dealt nothing), never spent by a skill. *Why the rename: the streak it needed is counted for every unit by the engine already, so the skill reads that instead of owning a second counter. Do not re-add Perfect Form.*
- **Second Bloom** *(Thousand Petals partner, exclusive)* — **BUILT.** `OnKill` **→ Thousand Petals** grants **7 more dance charges**, genuinely lengthening the storm (the beat loop reads the LIVE pile each beat). It grants **no Parry charges**, so an extended dance runs past its guard — deliberate, and the reason a long dance is a gamble.
- **Bare Branch** *(Returning Wind partner)* — **COMPOSED 2026-08-24, in a NEW shape.** `OnLowHP` → **arms 5 more Brace charges**. The old shape (the parry chain stops decaying) died with the chain itself; this one is a real partner again — the moment you are nearly dead is the moment you can hold more hits, and every held hit makes the Returning Wind release bigger. *Engine note: it adds stacks to Held Breath's own pile, so a charge is defined by Held Breath and never invented here; it works whether or not a Held Breath is already running.*
- **Eye of the Storm** — **COMPOSED 2026-08-24, in a NEW shape.** `OnParry` → **MP returns at 2/s for 4s** (8 MP, about one reaction skill). The old shape — "your next skill costs 0 MP" — has no mechanism behind it and would need a whole free-cast system; this keeps the meaning (a good read pays for the next one) using the MP-regen sub-stat that already exists.
- **Deep Roots** — `OnSkillCastComplete` **→ Still Air** grants **immunity to disables for 6s**, deliberately matching Still Air's own 6s (pairs with Single Petal's long wind-up).
- **Parting Clouds** *(combos with Bending Reed)* — **BUILT 2026-08-18, published.** **OnParry** → **a 50% chance** to grant **one charge of Boost Outgoing Damage at +200%, lasting 5s**. Your next attack is multiplied by it and spends it — **hit or miss**, because the charge pays for making the attack, not for landing it. The boost lands **before** the target's armour, so it is worth more the tankier the target. A combo passive (requires Bending Reed, whose parry is the moment it fires on): the read is the setup, the charge is the payoff. *(The 50% roll is what the asset holds and is the authority here — an earlier draft of this doc said the payoff was certain. Confirmed against the asset 2026-08-24.)*
  - *Engine note: no code names this skill. It composes from the* `OnParry` *trigger + a* `Boost Outgoing Damage` *effect. Bending Reed itself grants only its own charges — its earlier press-time boost was removed so the two can't stack.*
- **Headwind** — `OnDamageDealt` → cuts that target's HP regeneration by **3/s for 5s**, and it **STACKS** (one stack per qualifying hit, ceiling 1000), so sustained pressure stops their healing outright. **Published 2026-08-24.** Note the trigger carries **no run-of-hits gate**, so it is *every* hit you land, not a consecutive same-target streak — an earlier draft of this doc said "consecutive"; the asset says otherwise. A regen rate at or below zero pays nothing, so this can never drain a target. *Its bite on mobs still depends on the mob-regen system (see impl items).*
- **Crossed Branches** *(combos with Piercing Gust)* — `OnSkillCastComplete` **→ Piercing Gust** grants **5 charges** (tunable), each lasting **5s**: every basic attack you START **spends one charge to auto-lunge** the target again (it fires as the swing begins, even if the swing then misses), so it can't disengage while you keep swinging. **Each held charge also grants +2 basic attack range** (+10 at 5) — the charges are a stacking reach buff, so a full pile reaches further and shrinks by 2 with every lunge spent. **No Slow, no root** — the stickiness is on *you*, not a debuff on them. Out of charges, the passive does **nothing at all** (skill-level gate: no VFX, no lunge). Auto-lunges can't spend charges of their own — the no-self-feeding brake stops the chain. A combo passive (requires Piercing Gust, whose cast is what fills it); leans **Last Petal** (denies disengage while you build/cash Rising Breeze). *(Charges supersede the earlier duration-pin version, 2026-08-08 — see the **State System** doc, which owns the stacks/charges rules.)*

## Combos (setup → payoff)

- **Last Petal:** basic-attack to build Rising Breeze, staying on ONE target so **Tailwind** keeps compounding the run → Last Petal (cash it). Squall first to stack faster (fragile window).
- **Thousand Petals:** Squall (aspd → more charges, more parries, faster beat) → Thousand Petals, **Second Bloom** on every kill. The 2× damage taken is NOT suppressed any more; the Parry pile is your only cover.
- **Returning Wind:** Squall (2× incoming) → **Held Breath** (the doubled hits bank at doubled value, 0 HP now) → Returning Wind to release and erase the debt. **Bending Reed does NOT feed this** — a parried hit is erased before any damage number exists, so it banks nothing.

## Balance & implementation items (before/at build)

 1. **Attack Speed HARD CAP — DONE (2026-08-04).** `Character.AttackSpeedMult` (`Character.cs:370`) is now clamped by `MaxAttackSpeedMult = 3f` (`Character.cs:88`), whose comment cites Sword's Rising Breeze as the reason. Rising Breeze's equilibrium (attack rate × stack duration) is therefore bounded and Last Petal can't run away. Retune the `3f` value in playtest if the ceiling feels wrong.
 2. **Rising Breeze tuning:** stack **duration** vs base attack interval so low-aspd settles \~3–4 stacks and capped-aspd lands at the target ceiling; **% attack per stack** (\~3% start).
 3. **Auto-parry telegraph VFX** (S6).
 4. **Returning Wind — DONE.** Held Breath banks the hit instead of costing HP; the release is uncapped and erases the debt. Its gate demands **at least one banked hit**, so with an empty bank the ult does not fire at all — no animation, no cooldown, no mana.
 5. **Mob attack telegraph lead-time — DROPPED for this roster (2026-08-18).** A mob's melee swing begins, and its bite connects part way through it — at the same shared contact moment a person's swing lands at (70% of the swing) — and every screen draws the bite slamming in at that moment. That run-up is the only warning there is; no skill reads it. **Feint was the only skill that needed a tell, and Feint is cut**; Parting Clouds now rides Bending Reed, which reacts to the hit itself rather than to a tell. Any future telegraph-reading verb (on any weapon) has to be built on that run-up.
 6. **Healer mobs — CONTENT TODO.** Every unit now regenerates through the one shared ticker (see **Unified Unit Stats**), so **Headwind has something to cut** and is published. What is still missing is mobs that actively *heal* — the case where stopping regeneration is the difference between a kill and a stalemate.
 7. **Crossed Branches ↔ Piercing Gust combo — DONE (both assets composed).** Crossed Branches is a passive triggered by `OnSkillCastComplete` parameterized to **Piercing Gust**, granting **5 charges of +2 basic attack range, 5s each**; Piercing Gust carries the matching second trigger (`OnBasicAttackStart`, gated on that pile, spending one) so each basic attack you start auto-lunges. Cross-skill combo — Crossed Branches requires Piercing Gust, so it can only be bought once Piercing Gust has a point. Auto-pursue = the *caster* re-lunges; it is NOT a Slow/root on the target. Charge count still tunable.
 8. **Per-hit state/trigger order — LOCKED (from stress test).** One canonical order so combos resolve predictably every time: **(1)** hit lands → **(2)** **consume** one-shot states (Parting Clouds's charge, Stripped Bough's weaken charge) → **(3)** apply damage/effects → **(4)** roll new procs → **(5)** apply newly-granted states. Consume-before-grant means a strike **can't self-consume** a charge it just created.
 9. **OnBasicAttack recursion guard (code TODO).** Petal Storm and Rising Breeze both key off basic attacks; a basic attack **generated** by another (Petal Storm's flurry, Crossed Branches' auto-pursue) must **not** recursively spawn more basics unless explicitly flagged — cap the chain at one level or the flurry feeds itself.
10. **Taunt now carries its own rules — DONE (2026-08-24), and they are engine-wide, not Howling Wind's.** A taunted unit (1) is locked onto its taunter, (2) **cannot start any skill it chooses to use**, and (3) **has no pause between swings** — a ranged mob loses its standoff and closes in instead of kiting. Its swing RATE is untouched: the taunt removes gaps, never shortens the interval. The window moved out of the two hand-rolled timers (one in the mob brain, one in the player rig) into the shared state store, so all three rules hold for whoever the taunt lands on, whether or not that unit has any skills at all. **Passives still fire** — a taunt removes the target's CHOICES, and a passive is not one. **Not covered: forcing another PLAYER to attack in PvP** — steering an AI and taking control from a player are different systems.
11. **Crossed Branches "auto-pursue" — define ONE mechanism, engine-wide (code TODO).** "Your basic attacks automatically pursue" must resolve to a single behavior — **attack-lunge only** / **pathfind to target** / **forced facing** — chosen once so every future weapon reusing the primitive behaves identically. (Ties to #7.)

**Playtest watch-items (from stress test):** Squall trending universally-best (S3) · Returning Wind value swinging with enemy attack profile / biome (S5) · every build maxing Rising Breeze — verify the 3 ults still play differently (S7) · Still Air dead-slot confirmation (S4) · **Bending Reed→Parting Clouds 200% spike — watch it doesn't rival the ult burst path, and that the charge isn't wasted on a stray auto-attack before the big swing.**
