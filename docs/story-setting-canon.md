# Unwritten — Story & Setting (Canon)

The anchor lore doc. Everything else (world, mobs, classes, Ring of Progression, progression visuals) should be consistent with this. The core discovery: **the game's visual split — 3D charcoal players vs. flat 2D mobs — IS the story.** Nothing about the art is arbitrary.

## The premise

In ancient history, one person wrote down the **truth of the world** — a complete record of what was and what is. That book was then **corrupted by a negative entity**, which un-wrote it: tore the truth apart. The corrupted pages were scattered across the world, and where they landed they **transformed into monsters**.

With the record destroyed, **people lost their identities** — their color, their self, drained away, leaving them **charcoal-like husks**. Now they wander a seemingly endless journey, hunting the scattered pieces of paper, trying to **reconstruct the evidence of the past and recover the truth**.

The world is *Unwritten* — a world whose record was un-written by the entity.

## Why the art means what it means

- **2D flat mobs = corrupted pages.** They are literally paper. Flatness is not a style choice — it is what they are: fragments of the torn book given hostile form.
- **3D-but-charcoal players/NPCs = people who lost their identity.** The body is there (3D, present, real) but the ink has drained — no color, no self. The Quaternius charcoal-body + colored-hair + ink-eye rig telegraphs exactly this: a trace of identity clinging on.
- **Loot / drops = reclaimed fragments of the true text.** Killing a page = reading it = recovering a piece of what was un-written.
- **"Unwritten"** = the state of the world after the entity, and the quest to reverse it.

## Mechanical hooks this justifies (design directions, not commitments)

- **Charcoal → color as progression.** As a player reconstructs truth (levels, ring points, story beats), color returns to them — identity leaking back in. The rig already supports a gradient from charcoal husk toward a fully-colored self.
- **Killing a 2D mob = reading a page.** Drops can be *fragments* that assemble into recovered lore entries — a codex of the true world. Ties directly into loot + MapRecipe content.
- **Ring of Progression = a vessel that holds reclaimed identity/truth.** Tradeable, with lineage; the conservation economy fits — truth is *finite and recovered, never minted*. See the **Ring System — Current Facts (Trio-Ring)** doc (the tradeable-ring direction is recorded there as a proposed, uncommitted concept).
- **Bosses / the entity = concentrated corruption.** Larger, reassembled false-pages; the entity itself is the un-writer.
- **Conservation as balance driver.** Every gain pays a cost — mirrors "truth is recovered, not created."
