# A Pose Described in Words Needs a Picture First

**Reach for this when:** the user describes a body pose in words ("left foot forward", "blades crossing", "elbow near
the waist") and your second version still is not what they meant — or they say you cannot understand them.

## The rule

Words fix a few joints and leave the rest to you, and every joint you fill in can contradict what they pictured. Before
the first build, get a picture of the pose they have in mind (a reference image they point at, or one you find and
they approve). If none exists, say the pose back joint by joint (each foot, hip turn, lean, each elbow's bend and place,
each fist, each blade) in one list and wait for a yes, instead of building and letting the render be the question.

## Why

2026-10-09, the katar combat idle: five builds in a row, each one a fresh reading of the latest sentence, each one
breaking something the previous sentence had settled — blades forward when "up" was meant, crossing forced after the
user only allowed it, the left elbow never bent to 90° because nothing asked for it until the user said so. Every
render was measured and correct to its own keys; the keys were the guesses. The user: "im tired of you. you cannot
understand my instruction", then asked for reference images from another game.

## How to apply

- Ask for (or fetch) the reference before the first build. A game wiki's class art is usually a relaxed standing
  pose, not the fighting stance — say so and ask for a screenshot or video moment instead.
- With a reference in hand, read each limb from it the way the Ragnarok sprite cloning does: direction per joint, two
  views if possible.
- Without one, send the joint-by-joint list first; one line each, nothing invented beyond what they said marked as
  your choice.
