# Another Game's Rule, Quoted From Memory, Came Out Backwards

**Reach for this when:** you are about to back a design proposal with "this is how Ragnarok Online (or any other
game) does it", and you have not read that rule from a source this session.

## The rule

A rule of another game is a fact like any other: read it from a source (its wiki, its database, its formula) or do
not cite it. If you cannot check it, propose the design on its own merits and leave the other game out. This user
knows Ragnarok Online well and will catch a wrong claim at once, which costs the whole proposal its credibility.

## Why

2026-10-05: low-level creatures were swinging as fast as the player. I proposed that the held weapon set the swing
speed, "the way Ragnarok Online does it", with bare hands slow and weapons faster. Ragnarok Online is the opposite:
bare hands are the fastest and every weapon adds delay. The user corrected it, the proposal was dropped, and one
round was spent on a claim I never looked up.

## How to apply

- Before typing "like RO does" / "the way X works", ask: which page or table did I read that from, this session?
  No answer means delete the comparison.
- If the comparison matters to the proposal, look it up first (WebSearch the game's wiki) and quote what it says.
- If it does not matter, leave it out. The proposal stands or falls on the measured numbers of this game.
