# Creature Gear — What Every Creature Wears and Wields

> **Never append. Always update in place.** The last column of each row is what that creature's grade and folds
> multiply its pay by, under the rule in [What a Kill Pays](what-a-kill-pays.md).

## The rules

- **Every normal creature and elite wears a full set**: a weapon, a head piece, a body piece, shoes and a garment.
  How strong the set is comes from the creature's own grade and folds ([Mob Roster](mob-roster.md)).
- **Grade and folds are the only difficulty dial.** Level sets a creature's body; the grade and folds of its set
  decide how hard it is for that level. Neither follows its level.
- **Its grade follows the rarest thing it drops.** A normal creature with no rare material wears Raw; one with an
  Uncommon material wears F, a Rare one E, an Epic one D, a Legendary one C. An elite wears D for a Rare material,
  C for an Epic one, B for a Legendary one. The more a creature's drop is worth, the harder it is to take.
- **Its Ink follows its grade**: F Watery, E Thin, D Faint, C Clear, B Sharp. Raw writes nothing.
- **Its folds start at the limit of the grade below its own**, because a piece only reaches a grade by being folded
  through every grade under it: Raw starts at 0, F at 5, E at 10, D at 15, C at 20, B at 25. A piece with no folds
  gives nothing from folds whatever its grade, so a higher grade is never the weaker gear.
- **How hard it is to find adds up to 5 more folds**, rounded to the nearest fold, which reaches its own grade's
  limit. A normal creature gets all 5 at 10 or fewer alive in the whole world, three quarters of them at 11 to 20,
  half at 21 to 35, a quarter at 36 to 50, and none above 50. An elite gets all 5 on 1 map, three quarters on 2,
  half on 3, a quarter on 4, and none on 5.
- **The drop tiers, the numbers alive and the maps are those in [Creature Materials](creature-materials.md).**
- **Its armour follows what the creature is**: metal for shell, scale, horn, bone or bark; leather for hide, fur, feather or a fast body; cloth for soft, plant, flame or spirit.
- **Its weapon is the weapon of a town it lives around** — any town that holds a fifth or more of it. Where it has a
  choice it takes the weapon fewest creatures hold, so the weapons spread across the roster. A weapon decides how a
  creature attacks and which skills it can roll.
- **Every weapon is held by a normal creature, by an elite and by one shadow**, so each of the fourteen has a wielder
  of every kind. Toadmaw holds the Shuriken instead of Kharnel's Scythe, because the town rule alone left no elite
  with one. Each shadow holds the weapon it stands for ([Mob Roster](mob-roster.md)).
- **Its stats are built for its weapon.** Each weapon has a build, and a creature spends its level's whole stat budget
  in that build's proportions. A weapon with two builds gives the second to a creature in metal armour.
- **Its grade and its folds each multiply what it pays** ([What a Kill Pays](what-a-kill-pays.md)): its
  experience, its material prices and its saved-character gold. Its stat build and its armour material do not.

## The shadows

Every shadow wears a full set — head, body, shoes and garment — of one material, at grade A +30 with Sharp Ink. Its
weapon is the one it stands for, and its stats are spent in that weapon's build.

**Its armour is the material that buys what its weapon's mastery raises:** leather (attack speed and evasion) for a
weapon whose mastery raises attack speed or evasion; otherwise cloth (magic defence and mana) for one whose mastery
raises mana or magic attack; otherwise metal (defence and health). Asterion is the one exception: the Fist shadow
wears metal and takes the Fist's Guardian build (decided 2026-10-13). Between them the fourteen wear all twelve
armour pieces.

## Still open

- **Hazemere has no fields of its own.** It is a city joined by doors to Velmora and Tornel, so the Katar goes to four
  creatures of those two towns' fields. Its own fields are not laid out yet.
- **Lastline and Old Oak have no weapon of their own**; the creatures around them carry the Sword.
- **The grade ladder and the fold shares are first guesses**: which grade each drop tier gives, and the five
  bands of how many are alive.
- **A low-level creature in high-grade gear is far harder for its level than a high-level one in the same gear**,
  because a fold gives the same amount at every level. Gloop King, level 8, wears C +23.
- **A weapon's skill is rolled fresh each life**, and what a creature pays does not count it.
- **Each armour material is a first reading of the creature's portrait, and each build's shares are first guesses.**
