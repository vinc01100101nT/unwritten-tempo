# Saved Characters — When a Mob Transforms Back

> **Never append. Always update in place.** This page states only what has been AGREED. Anything still being
> decided lives in **Still open** at the bottom and nowhere else.

Every 2D mob is a page of the artist's book that Chaos corrupted. Beating one can turn it back into the character the
artist really drew — the 2D character sprites. The character is cured and has free will: some thank you and leave,
some fight beside you for a moment, and some choose to fight you. The lore is in
[Story & Setting (Canon)](story-setting-canon.md).

## When a mob transforms back

- **15% of kills** of a normal or an elite transform. A shadow never transforms.
- The mob's own drops fall as they always do, at its death. The character appears where it died.
- **An elite always becomes the same ninja** (the table below), and every ninja is a rebel.
- **A normal becomes a random character** from every character except the ninjas, who belong to the elites. The same
  character can come from any normal, at any level.
- **The character is the mob's level**, and a rebel gives the experience a mob of that level gives.
- When it appears it says one line in a speech bubble, drawn at random from its behaviour's set (below).

## The three behaviours

| Behaviour | What it does | Gold | Items | Experience |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Never fights** | Stays on its spot, says its line, and after **30 seconds** says a goodbye line and vanishes. | Pays its saver gold (below). | Its creature's own material (below). | — |
| **Ally** | Fights on its saver's side for **30 seconds**, then says a goodbye line and vanishes. It attacks mobs, rebels, and anything that treats its saver as an enemy. | — | — | — |
| **Rebel** | Attacks straight away, with the same aggression as a mob: it is not tied to the player who freed it. If nobody beats it within **30 seconds**, it leaves. | None. | Drops loot **one kind up**: a rebel from a normal drops as an elite does, a rebel from an elite drops as a shadow does. | As a mob of its level. |

**Sides are a general feature, not an ally special case.** Every unit belongs to a side, and anything that treats a
player as an enemy — a mob, a rebel, and later a player in PvP — is an enemy of that player's allies too.

## Gold

- **A mob's death pays no gold.** Normals, elites and shadows all stop paying gold on death.
- **A never-fights character pays the player who saved it**, the moment it appears: gold, and some of its creature's
  own ordinary material. Only a normal can become one.
- **The gold is worth a number of kills of that creature**, at 10 gold a level of the level its file gives it — never
  the level it grew to in the fight — times its gear's grade multiplier and fold multiplier ([What a Kill Pays](what-a-kill-pays.md)). The saver's run of kills
  multiplies the gold, as it does a kill's experience.
- **The material goes into the saver's bag**, and whatever the bag has no room for falls where the character stands,
  kept for the saver like any drop. The run of kills does not multiply it.

| Tier | Gold worth | Material | Together worth | Characters |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Wealthy** | 12 kills | 9 | 30 kills | Princess, Noble, Sultan, SultanWhite |
| **Helpless** | 8 kills | 6 | 20 kills | Child, Boy, EggBoy, EggGirl, OldMan, OldManCap, OldManStraw, OldWoman |
| **Ordinary** | 4 kills | 3 | 10 kills | Villager, VillagerBlond, VillagerCap, VillagerGreen, VillagerRedCap, VillagerSpiky, Woman, Eskimo, Hunter, Inspector, ManGreen |

**What that comes to:** a normal kill turns back (15%) into one of the 23 never-fights characters out of the 73 a normal
can become — 1 kill in about 21. One ordinary material sells for two kills' worth, so over many kills a rescue adds
about 0.8 of a kill's worth to every kill: roughly 44% of all the gold hunting earns, arriving as rare large sums,
with selling what creatures drop as the larger part. Prices and chances: [Creature Materials](creature-materials.md).

### Why a cured character still fights you

- **They refuse the pen.** They watched the artist's paranoia turn the Fourteen, and they will not be written again.
  You carry his ink. *(The ninjas, and the rebels among the tribe, warriors and beast folk.)*
- **They were drawn as villains.** Even a gentle book has its rivals and pranksters; cured, they choose to play the
  part they were drawn for. *(The storybook villains.)*
- **They were left unfinished.** Characters the artist abandoned half-drawn resent him, and you work for him.
  *(The constructs.)*

## What they say

One line is drawn at random, by the server, from the set for the moment and the behaviour, and every player nearby
reads the same words in a speech bubble. There are eight sets — never-fights appearing (8 lines) and leaving (6), an
ally appearing (8) and leaving (6), a rebel appearing for each of its three reasons (7, 6 and 5), and a rebel being
beaten (6). **The lines live in the game, in `unity/Assets/Scripts/SavedCharacterLines.cs`, and nowhere else**; edit
them there.

## How it plays, moment by moment

- **Appearing:** the character stands where the mob fell, wearing its own picture at a person's height and **half its
  picture's width**, with the name of its asset (its folder name, spaced — "Villager Red Cap"). For its first **2
  seconds** an aura, drawn at half its own size, marks what it is, then fades out: the love aura for one that never
  fights, the star aura for an ally, the debuff aura for a rebel. Its portrait shows beside its name when you point at
  it or fight it, as every creature's does. A never-fights character pays its saver the moment it appears.
- **Its poses**, from the three rows under its walk: the attack picture for the way it faces when it swings; while a
  skill winds up, the second picture of the last row; when the skill is let go, the third — or the fourth, for a skill
  with no wind-up; and the first picture of the last row, the dead one, as it falls. (A mob's sheet has no such rows,
  so mobs are unchanged.)
- **Leaving:** after **30 seconds** it stops fighting (nobody can hit it and it hits nobody). A never-fights character
  or an ally says its goodbye line and vanishes **2 seconds** later so the line can be read; a rebel has no goodbye
  and goes at once.
- **An ally** fights around where it appeared; it does not follow its saver. Whatever it beats is its saver's kill —
  the experience, the loot and the chance to turn back.
- **A rebel** stays until it is beaten or its 30 seconds are up. It never summons, even as an elite's ninja.
- **The same creature, redrawn:** a saved character is a body of the very mob that fell, so it keeps that mob's stats,
  gear, element and bought skills at the level it had when it died; only its picture, its size, its side and what it pays
  change.

## Still open

- **First guesses, to be tuned in play:** the 4 / 8 / 12 kills of gold and the 3 / 6 / 9 materials a rescue pays, the 2 seconds a goodbye stays readable, and
  how long a swing (0.3 seconds) and a release (0.4 seconds) picture stays on screen.
- **Which mixed characters are allies and which are rebels** is a first pass by look: mostly, the re-coloured twin of a
  pair is the rebel.
- **The names shown** are the art folder names, spaced, until real names are chosen; each is one field on the
  character's asset.
- **Calls made while building, each a one-line change if wrong:** a character's strength is its mob's; the rescue gold is
  paid as the character appears; an ally's kills are its saver's; a rebel from an elite never summons; a goodbye line is
  readable for 2 seconds before the character vanishes.
- **The dialog lines** are a first draft.
