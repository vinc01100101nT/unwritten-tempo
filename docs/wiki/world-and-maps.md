# World & Maps

*Where the pages fell.*

## A world of scattered pages

When the truth was un-written, its pages scattered everywhere — so the whole world is a hunting ground. Zones are built from **biomes**, each with its own look, ground, and the monster families that settled there.

## The biomes

- **Grassland** — the gentle starting country.
- **Snow** — frozen highlands.
- **Desert** — arid wastes.
- **Volcanic** — scorched, fiery ground.
- **Corrupted** — where the un-writing bit deepest; the most twisted pages gather here.

Each biome carries a **themed set of monsters**, grouped by difficulty tier, so a zone's danger and its inhabitants match its biome automatically.
