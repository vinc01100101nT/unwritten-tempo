# Monsters

*The bestiary — the corrupted pages you hunt.*

## What monsters are

Every monster in Unwritten is a **corrupted page** of the world's lost truth, given hostile form. That's why they appear **flat and papery**: their two-dimensional look isn't a style choice — it's what they *are*. To defeat one is, in a sense, to **read** the page it came from and recover a fragment of what was un-written.

## How monsters are organized

- **By biome** — each biome (Grassland, Snow, Desert, Volcanic, Corrupted) has its own family of monsters. See *World & Maps*.
- **By difficulty tier** — within a biome, monsters range from fodder up to tougher elites, so a zone's challenge scales with where it sits.
- **By size** — monsters are **Small, Medium, or Large**, which is meant to interact with your weapon type (see *Combat Mechanics*).

## Bosses & the entity

- **Bosses** are larger, reassembled clusters of false pages — concentrated, deliberate corruption.
- **The entity** is the un-writer itself: the force that tore the truth apart in the first place, and the ultimate source of every monster.
