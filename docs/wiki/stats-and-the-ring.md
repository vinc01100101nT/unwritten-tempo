# Stats & the Ring

*How you grow — the six base stats, and the Ring that scales your power.*

## Primary stats

Six base stats you allocate as you level — **anyone can raise any of them** (there are no classes):

- **STR** — physical attack power
- **AGI** — attack speed and dodge
- **VIT** — health and defense
- **INT** — magic power and mana
- **DEX** — accuracy and cast speed
- **LUK** — critical chance and luck

## The Ring — your growth engine

The Ring is a **closed loop of ten percentage-based passive stats** that you build up as you level. It's a separate layer from your weapon: your **weapon grants your skills**, and the **Ring scales their power**. Your progress on the Ring is saved with your character.

### The ten Ring stats

Going clockwise around the loop:

**Defense → HP → Attack → Accuracy → Cast Speed → Magic Attack → MP → Evasion → Critical → Attack Speed →** *(back to Defense)*

The two **poles sit dead-opposite: Defense (top) and Magic Attack (bottom)**, with the survival stats bridging them — so drifting from a physical toward a magic build naturally carries you across the defenses in between.

Two stats do double duty: **Defense** raises both your physical *and* magic defense (there is no separate magic-defense stat), and **Critical** raises both your crit rate *and* your crit damage.

### The draft — a steering wheel

Every level-up you pick **one of four** options:

- **Three known** — your current stat plus its **two neighbors** on the ring (a sliding window of three).
- **One wildcard** — a random stat from elsewhere on the ring.

Picking the center keeps stacking that stat; picking a neighbor slides your window that way; the wildcard jumps you across the ring.

### Percentage growth with a soft cap

Ring stats give **percentage multipliers**, not flat points, so they never go obsolete as your gear grows. Growth follows a **soft cap**: most stats climb toward a **+80% ceiling**, while **Critical caps at +50%** and **Evasion at +60%** (both would break combat if left uncapped). Because each extra rank is worth a little less, the math **rewards spreading out** — though single-stacking is always allowed.

## Your Ring locks your role

**Skills don't level up** — their power comes entirely from your Ring. And that scaling is role-bound: **healing scales off MP**, **damage scales off Attack / Magic Attack**. So a support who invested MP hits like wet paper with a damage weapon, and a damage build heals for crumbs with a staff. **Swapping weapons swaps your skills — never your power**, which stays bound to the stats you chose.

*(Ring upgrades that add whole new behaviors from investment are a planned future addition — for now the Ring only scales numbers.)*

## The Ring as your life's work *(proposed — not committed)*

A larger direction under consideration — **not final** — would make the Ring an actual **tradeable in-world item** that holds your whole progression, carrying a visible **lineage of past bearers**. It is a design concept only, not in the game today.
