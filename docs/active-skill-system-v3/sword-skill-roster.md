# Sword — Skill Roster (V3)

The first fully-authored weapon. Companion to **Weapon Roster — 12 Identities** (identity litmus), **Active Skill System — V3** (channels/effects/scaling), **Skill Numbers & Balance Baseline** (bands), and **State System** (buffs/debuffs/marks). Converged over a long ult-first design pass + a GPT stress-test round.

**Identity:** *Win duels through precise single-target bursts and timed defense.* Might / Atk · Burst. Everything is **single-target**.

**Hard bans (identity walls):** no **crit**, no **armor-pierce** (crit is Finesse's identity — and in our engine crit is the *only* thing that ignores DEF, so giving Sword crit hands it Finesse's whole mechanic), no **AoE / DoT / heal / execute**. **Precision = TIMING (Parry), never crit.**

## Design guide — the universal-but-specialized rule (heuristic, not law)

**Every ult should have at least one normal that *specializes* in it while still benefiting the other two ults.** This builds a shared spine so the three ult-builds aren't siloed mini-kits. Rules:

- The specialization must be **asymmetric and real** — one ult clearly gains the most, or the skill is filler.
- Cap it at **~1 per ult**; keep the *other* normals **sharply single-purpose** (else the roster blurs into "lean-buffs").
- It's a **heuristic, not a law** — don't force the resource→specialist→ult shape onto weapons whose identity doesn't want it (e.g. Firearm may need only one setup skill).
- **Not every normal must be build-optimal.** Some weaker "trash-ish" slots are intentional — players discover which rolled skills suit their build. A perfectly flat roster where every skill is equally good is *not* the goal.

Sword's spine: **Momentum → Deathblow · Brace → Retribution · Overclock → Blade Dance.** (Blade Dance is no longer an ult — see below.)

## Momentum — Deathblow's partner (a ROLLABLE passive skill, NOT default)

**Momentum is one of the Sword's passive-pool skills — a skill you *roll*, not intrinsic weapon behavior.** It's Deathblow's partner in the spine (a partner may be an **active or a passive** — Parry/Overclock are active partners, Momentum is the passive one). A Sword only has Momentum if it rolled this passive.

- **Consequence (LOCKED — Option A):** a Deathblow that *didn't* roll Momentum is a flat **~600% ATK** nuke with **no** +40%/stack scaling. That's accepted loot-combo tension — a Deathblow build *chases* Momentum; bad-luck protection (see **Weapon Crafting & Economy**) cushions the miss. Deathblow stays usable without it, just doesn't scale.

- **Generated by successful basic attacks** (+1 stack each). **Not** from any active skill — this is why Attack Speed matters.
- V3 State: **Private, Stacking, NO stack cap**, each stack its own duration, **FIFO** expiry, **no refresh exceptions**.
- Each stack = **+X% attack** (~**+3%/stack** baseline) applied to **all** outgoing damage — basics, actives, all three ults.
- **Equilibrium, not infinity:** because stacks expire on FIFO timers, your stable count ≈ **attack rate × stack duration**. Attack Speed is the lever; the new **Attack Speed cap** (below) bounds the ceiling so it can't run away.
- **Deathblow double-dips Momentum by design** (its specialty) — see below.
- **State-order (LOCKED, closes the S2 ambiguity):** on Deathblow cast → **snapshot stack count → compute *both* Deathblow's +40%/stack scaling AND the standing attack% off that snapshot → deal damage → THEN consume all stacks.** Never consume before reading.

## Ultimates — ⚠️ NOW TWO, NOT THREE (2026-08-16)

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Deathblow** | Execution spike | One massive hit; **consumes all Momentum** | Base **600% ATK + 40%/stack** (stacks uncapped) **×** the standing Momentum attack% (deliberate double-dip) · 1s cast | **Momentum** |
| **Retribution** | Reactive counter | Cash the damage **Brace** delayed: a plain strike scaled off the banked total, and **the debt is erased** — you never pay what you release | Release = **banked × 2.5** · **NO CAP** · plain strike (no reflect recursion) | **Brace** |

Design a third, or ship with two? Nothing else in this doc has been restructured for that.

## Active skills (target 9 — 8 locked, 1 open)

Two are **spine partners** (carry an ult); the rest are single-purpose texture.

**Brace** *(Retribution's spine partner)* — *Take it now, pay it later — or don't pay at all.* Arms **5 charges for 4s**. Each charge holds back ONE incoming hit: instead of costing HP, that hit's damage is **banked**, and a cold-blue number pops for it. Out of charges, the next hit lands normally. **10 MP · 7s cd.**

- **The bill comes due when the window ends** — everything banked lands at once as a single **true-damage** hit (its own purple number). Brace DELAYS damage, it never prevents it.
- **What's banked is what would really have cost HP:** Guard, Vulnerability and Shield all apply *before* the hit is recorded, and a parried hit never lands at all, so it banks nothing. Poison/bleed/burn ticks bypass it entirely.
- **Its only escape is Retribution** (below). Cashing the pile erases the debt — that IS Retribution's perk. With the ult uncharged, bracing is a bet you will lose.
- **The cooldown deliberately exceeds the window** (7s vs 4s), so a second cast can't chain-postpone the bill forever.
- *Engine note: two states — the armed charges (spend down, can hit zero) and the debt (must outlive them). Parry's exact shape, one word apart: a parry **erases** the hit, a brace **postpones** it.*

**Parry** — *Read the hit and punish it.* Blocks the next hit fully (**0 HP**), grants **next-strike +50%** (any ult → helps all three).
- ⚠️ **STALE — needs a design decision (2026-08-16).** Everything below was written when Retribution owned its own brace and Parry was its spine partner. **Brace is now a separate skill and Retribution's partner**, and the two do not interact: a parried hit is erased before any damage number exists, so it **banks nothing**. The auto-parry chain, the release bonus and the compounding tower described below are all unimplemented and currently unreachable. Parry today is exactly its first line — a full negate, plus a next-strike bonus that is itself still unbuilt. **Decide whether Parry gets a new role or keeps standing alone, then rewrite this block.**

**Overclock** (was "Flurry") *(Blade Dance's spine partner)* — *Go faster, fragile.* **+30% Attack Speed for 8s, but take 2× damage for 3s.** The 2× drawback **flips per build**:
- **Blade Dance** (specialist): attack speed buys **more charges AND more parries** (both counts are derived from your swing rate), and it beats faster. ⚠️ **The old "its full invuln suppresses the 2×" no longer holds** — Blade Dance grants no invulnerability, so the 2× is a real cost you take with parries as the only cover. Reads as a bigger, more honest gamble than it used to.
- **Retribution:** 2× incoming → **2× bank** — drawback becomes upside.
- **Deathblow:** aspd → faster basics → Momentum quicker, but fragile for 3s.
- 12 MP · 6s cd. *(Watch-item: trends universally desirable; the 2× cost is the governor for every build now.)*

**Blade Dance** *(Overclock's specialist — BUILT 2026-08-16)* — *Become the storm.* A **base-tier active**: **10 MP · 5s cd · 11 range**. On cast it hands you **two piles that both last 8s and are both sized by your swing rate** — dance charges (`ceil(8 ÷ your seconds-per-swing)`) and **half that many, rounded up, in Parry charges**. Then it beats: **3 beats per swing**, each beat **spends one dance charge**, hops to the next of **7 star corners** leaving a **1s afterimage**, and strikes for **100% ATK**. Each beat re-aims at a random living mob within **3 units of the mob it just struck**, so the dance walks target to target; nothing in range ends it. **One beat, one target — this is single-target, never AoE.**
- Each held dance charge is also **+1% damage** (they are a stacking Damage buff), so the storm is strongest at its start and tapers as it spends down.
- **Defence is the Parry pile, not invulnerability** — it erases hits until it runs out, then you are exposed. Deliberate: a defence you can drain is a decision, not a freebie.
- **Measured consequence of the current tuning:** at base speed that is **14 slashes over ~2.8s** (14 × 0.2s) while the **parries last the full 8s** — so the guard outlives the dance by ~5s. Set beats-per-swing to 1 if you want the dance to fill its own window instead.
- *Engine note: no code names this skill. It composes from `repeatCostsCharge` + `repeatScaleWithAttackSpeed` + `repeatReaim` + attack-speed-derived stack counts + the authored star corner count. See the implementation notes.*

**Lunge** — *Close the gap and strike.* Dash to a target + strike. The "reach" glue. 100% ATK · 8 MP · 5s cd · reach **8 world units** (`castRange`; the unit every range in this doc is written in — not navigation cells, which are 0.5 each). **Combo:** if you also rolled **Lock Blades**, casting Lunge grants it **5 charges** (tunable) — a Lunge+Lock Blades build keeps a near-constant supply of auto-lunges.

**Guard Stance** — *Weather it.* Take **50% less damage for 5s** (Guard, Def-scaled). 12 MP · 10s cd. **Intentionally the weakest slot** (dominated by Parry/Blade Dance, anti-synergizes Retribution) — kept as the always-available sustain floor and a deliberate "not every roll is optimal" slot.

**Feint** — *Strike between heartbeats.* A quick strike; landing it on an enemy **mid-attack-windup** **interrupts and staggers** the attack. The telegraph-read verb — distinct from Parry (which negates the hit). **Combo:** if you also rolled **Opening**, a successful Feint has a chance to grant an *Opening* state (see passives) — Feint becomes a setup→payoff verb. *Needs mob telegraph lead-time (see impl items).*

**Committed Strike** — *Wind up and commit.* A slow, telegraphed **heavy single hit** — the pool's one true **damage-typed** skill (Physical `damageMult`). Pairs with **Unwavering** (guard up so nothing shoves you out of the wind-up).

**Disarm** — *Blunt their swing.* A strike that briefly **weakens the target's next attack** (cuts its outgoing damage). It weakens, it does **not** deny/stun — stays clear of Warhammer. Anti-burst tool when Retribution is on cooldown.

**Onslaught** — *Unleash a flurry.* An instant burst of several **real basic attacks** (no skill `damageMult`; ATK-based) — so it feeds **Momentum** and rides every OnBasicAttack passive. Distinct from Overclock (a sustained *buff*); Onslaught is an on-demand Momentum dump.

**[OPEN — 1 more active]** — to design (Stage-2 mini-loop). Single-purpose duelist verb, no clone, bans respected.

## Passive skills (9)

Behavioral, event-triggered (via the trigger system Momentum proves). **Each ult has a special passive partner** — some universal-but-specialized, some ult-exclusive (fine: passives are rolled per build, so an exclusive one is build-defining, not dead).

- **Momentum** *(Deathblow partner)* — see its own section above. OnBasicAttack → stacks; +%ATK to all damage; Deathblow consumes.
- **Perfect Form** *(Deathblow partner)* — same-target hit streak: **+1 Momentum-per-hit every 3 consecutive same-target hits** *and* slows stack decay; **resets on miss/target-swap**. (Merged the old "Duelist's Focus".)
- **Encore** *(Blade Dance partner, exclusive)* — **OnKill during Blade Dance** → extend the storm. Only functions mid-Blade Dance. **NOT BUILT, but the engine now supports it with no special case:** the beat loop reads the LIVE charge pile each beat, so a passive that grants more dance charges genuinely lengthens the run. Still to decide: how many charges per kill, and whether it also grants Parry charges (today it would not, so an extended dance runs past its guard).
- **Last Stand** *(Retribution partner)* — **OnLowHP**: while below the HP threshold, Retribution's **parry-chain doesn't decay** (75/75/75… instead of 75/50/25) — a real low-HP comeback, no added numbers.
- **Composure** — **OnParrySuccess** → your next skill costs **0 MP**.
- **Unwavering** — **while Guard Stance active** → immune to knockback/interrupt (pairs with Committed Strike's long wind-up).
- **Opening** *(combos with Feint)* — **OnFeintSuccess** → **chance to grant a private *Opening* state**; your **next damaging strike consumes it for +200% ATK** (a bonus single-target **Physical** hit — no crit, no pierce, ban-clean). The proc **chance scales with the Accuracy ring stat** (authored as a `chance`-channel affinity weight — data-layer only, deliberately **not** shown in the player tooltip). A combo passive (needs Feint rolled to fire), turning Feint into a setup→payoff verb. **State order:** an existing Opening is **consumed before** Feint rolls a new one (see impl items) — a hit can't self-consume the Opening it just created. *(Replaces the old telegraph-reveal Opening — no longer depends on mob telegraphs.)*
- **Pressure** — **OnDamageDealt** (consecutive, same target) → **delays that target's HP regeneration**. *Depends on the mob-regen system (see impl items).*
- **Lock Blades** *(combos with Lunge)* — **`OnSkillCastComplete` → Lunge** grants **5 charges** (tunable), each lasting **5s**: each hit you land **spends one charge to auto-lunge** the target again, so it can't disengage while you keep connecting. **Each held charge also grants +5% Attack Speed** (+25% at 5) — the charges are a stacking attack-speed buff, so a full pile both chases *and* swings faster, feeding Momentum while it lasts. **No Slow, no root** — the stickiness is on *you*, not a debuff on them. Out of charges, the passive does **nothing at all** (skill-level gate: no VFX, no lunge). Auto-lunges can't spend charges of their own — the no-self-feeding brake stops the chain. A combo passive (needs Lunge rolled to fire); leans **Deathblow** (denies disengage while you build/cash Momentum). *(Charges supersede the earlier duration-pin version, 2026-08-08 — see the **State System** doc, which owns the stacks/charges rules.)*

## Combos (setup → payoff)

- **Deathblow:** basic-attack to build Momentum → Deathblow (cash it). Overclock first to stack faster (fragile window).
- **Blade Dance:** Overclock (aspd → more charges, more parries, faster beat) → Blade Dance. The 2× damage taken is NOT suppressed any more; the Parry pile is your only cover.
- **Retribution:** Overclock (2× incoming) → Retribution brace → Parry the big hits (bank doubled, 0 HP) → release.

## Balance & implementation items (before/at build)

1. **Attack Speed HARD CAP — DONE (2026-08-04).** `Character.AttackSpeedMult` (`Character.cs:370`) is now clamped by `MaxAttackSpeedMult = 3f` (`Character.cs:88`), whose comment cites Sword's Momentum as the reason. Momentum's equilibrium (attack rate × stack duration) is therefore bounded and Deathblow can't run away. Retune the `3f` value in playtest if the ceiling feels wrong.
2. **Momentum tuning:** stack **duration** vs base attack interval so low-aspd settles ~3–4 stacks and capped-aspd lands at the target ceiling; **% attack per stack** (~3% start).
3. **Auto-parry telegraph VFX** (S6).
4. **Retribution:** brace costs HP and banks it; release uncapped.
5. **Mob attack telegraph lead-time — NEW (code TODO).** Measured: billboard mobs' `AttackLunge` squash currently fires *at* impact. **Feint** and reactive defense need mobs to telegraph ~0.2–0.3s **before** the hit — shift the tell earlier. *(Opening no longer needs this — it keys off Feint's success, not a mob tell.)*
6. **Mob HP regen + healer mobs — NEW SYSTEM (code TODO).** Measured: mobs have no regen today (only `leashRadius`). **Pressure** requires a mob HP-regen system (and some mobs with heal skills) to have anything to delay. Build it, then Pressure activates.
7. **Lock Blades ↔ Lunge combo (CONTENT TODO — engine built).** Lock Blades is authored as a passive triggered by `OnSkillCastComplete` parameterized to **Lunge**, granting **5 charges** (tune the count) that each also carry +5% Attack Speed; each landed hit spends one to auto-lunge. Cross-skill combo — only fires if both are rolled. Auto-pursue = the *caster* re-lunges; it is NOT a Slow/root on the target. The machinery it needs (stacks/charges, the skill-level gate, consume-per-use, the no-self-feeding brake) is **built** — what remains is authoring the skill asset.
8. **Per-hit state/trigger order — LOCKED (from stress test).** One canonical order so combos resolve predictably every time: **(1)** hit lands → **(2)** **consume** one-shot states (Opening, Composure's 0-MP flag, Parry's next-strike empower) → **(3)** apply damage/effects → **(4)** roll new procs → **(5)** apply newly-granted states. Consequence (the Opening case): consume-before-grant means a strike **can't self-consume** the Opening it just created.
9. **OnBasicAttack recursion guard (code TODO).** Onslaught and Momentum both key off basic attacks; a basic attack **generated** by another (Onslaught's flurry, Lock Blades' auto-pursue) must **not** recursively spawn more basics unless explicitly flagged — cap the chain at one level or the flurry feeds itself.
10. **Lock Blades "auto-pursue" — define ONE mechanism, engine-wide (code TODO).** "Your basic attacks automatically pursue" must resolve to a single behavior — **attack-lunge only** / **pathfind to target** / **forced facing** — chosen once so every future weapon reusing the primitive behaves identically. (Ties to #7.)
