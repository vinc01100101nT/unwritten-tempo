# Sword — Skill Roster (V3)

The first fully-authored weapon. Companion to **Weapon Roster — 12 Identities** (identity litmus), **Active Skill System — V3** (channels/effects/scaling), **Skill Numbers & Balance Baseline** (bands), and **State System** (buffs/debuffs/marks). Converged over a long ult-first design pass + a GPT stress-test round.

**Identity:** *Win duels through precise single-target bursts and timed defense.* Might / Atk · Burst. Everything is **single-target**.

**Hard bans (identity walls):** no **crit**, no **armor-pierce** (crit is Finesse's identity — and in our engine crit is the *only* thing that ignores DEF, so giving Sword crit hands it Finesse's whole mechanic), no **AoE / DoT / heal / execute**. **Precision = TIMING (Parry), never crit.**

## Design guide — the universal-but-specialized rule (heuristic, not law)

**Every ult should have at least one normal that *specializes* in it while still benefiting the other two ults.** This builds a shared spine so the three ult-builds aren't siloed mini-kits. Rules:

- The specialization must be **asymmetric and real** — one ult clearly gains the most, or the skill is filler.
- Cap it at **\~1 per ult**; keep the *other* normals **sharply single-purpose** (else the roster blurs into "lean-buffs").
- It's a **heuristic, not a law** — don't force the resource→specialist→ult shape onto weapons whose identity doesn't want it (e.g. Firearm may need only one setup skill).
- **Not every normal must be build-optimal.** Some weaker "trash-ish" slots are intentional — players discover which rolled skills suit their build. A perfectly flat roster where every skill is equally good is *not* the goal.

Sword's spine: **Momentum → Deathblow · Brace → Retribution · Overclock → Blade Dance.** All three payoffs are authored `tier = Ultimate` in the assets.

## Momentum — Deathblow's partner (a ROLLABLE passive skill, NOT default)

**Momentum is one of the Sword's passive-pool skills — a skill you *roll*, not intrinsic weapon behavior.** It's Deathblow's partner in the spine (a partner may be an **active or a passive** — Parry/Overclock are active partners, Momentum is the passive one). A Sword only has Momentum if it rolled this passive.

- **Consequence (LOCKED — Option A):** a Deathblow that *didn't* roll Momentum is a flat **\~600% ATK** nuke with **no** +40%/stack scaling. That's accepted loot-combo tension — a Deathblow build *chases* Momentum; bad-luck protection (see **Weapon Crafting & Economy**) cushions the miss. Deathblow stays usable without it, just doesn't scale.

- **Generated by successful basic attacks** (+1 stack each). **Not** from any active skill — this is why Attack Speed matters.

- V3 State: **Private, Stacking**, each stack its own duration, **FIFO** expiry, **no refresh exceptions**. **As composed: 5s per stack, ceiling 99** — high enough that the equilibrium below, not the ceiling, is what bounds it.

- Each stack = **+3% damage** (as composed) applied to **all** outgoing damage — basics, actives, all three ults.

- **Equilibrium, not infinity:** because stacks expire on FIFO timers, your stable count ≈ **attack rate × stack duration**. Attack Speed is the lever; the new **Attack Speed cap** (below) bounds the ceiling so it can't run away.

- **Deathblow double-dips Momentum by design** (its specialty) — see below.

- **State-order (LOCKED, closes the S2 ambiguity):** on Deathblow cast → **snapshot stack count → compute *both* Deathblow's +40%/stack scaling AND the standing attack% off that snapshot → deal damage → THEN consume all stacks.** Never consume before reading.

## Ultimates — THREE, as composed (re-confirmed 2026-08-24 off the assets)

All three are authored `tier = Ultimate` and priced as ultimates (**40 MP**, long cooldown, no charge meter — see **Skill Numbers & Balance Baseline**, cost & cooldown rules).

| Ult | Payoff | Effect | Numbers (as composed) | Specialist feeder |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Deathblow** | Execution spike | One massive hit; **consumes all Momentum** | **600% ATK + 40%/stack** **×** the standing Momentum damage% (deliberate double-dip) · 40 MP · 60s cd · 1s cast · range 5 | **Momentum** |
| **Retribution** | Reactive counter | Cash the damage **Brace** delayed, and **the debt is erased** — you never pay what you release | **100% ATK + 250% of the banked total**, split across **5 strikes** · **NO CAP** · plain strike (no reflect recursion) · 40 MP · 45s cd · range 11 | **Brace** |
| **Blade Dance** | Sustained storm | Two attack-speed-sized piles, then a beat that walks target to target | Dance charges = `ceil(8 ÷ seconds-per-swing)`; Parry charges = half that (`stackShare 0.5`); both **8s**. **3 beats per swing**, each spends one dance charge, hops a **7-corner star** with a **1s afterimage**, strikes **100% ATK**, re-aims within **3 units**. ## Active skills (10 composed, none open)

Two are **spine partners** (carry an ult); the rest are single-purpose texture.

**Brace** *(Retribution's spine partner)* — *Take it now, pay it later — or don't pay at all.* Arms **5 charges for 4s**. Each charge holds back ONE incoming hit: instead of costing HP, that hit's damage is **banked**, and a cold-blue number pops for it. Out of charges, the next hit lands normally. **12 MP · 7s cd.**

- **The bill comes due when the window ends** — everything banked lands at once as a single **true-damage** hit (its own purple number). Brace DELAYS damage, it never prevents it.
- **What's banked is what would really have cost HP:** Guard, Vulnerability and Shield all apply *before* the hit is recorded, and a parried hit never lands at all, so it banks nothing. Poison/bleed/burn ticks bypass it entirely.
- **Its only escape is Retribution** (below). Cashing the pile erases the debt — that IS Retribution's perk. With the ult uncharged, bracing is a bet you will lose.
- **The cooldown deliberately exceeds the window** (7s vs 4s), so a second cast can't chain-postpone the bill forever.
- *Engine note: two states — the armed charges (spend down, can hit zero) and the debt (must outlive them). Parry's exact shape, one word apart: a parry **erases** the hit, a brace **postpones** it.*

**Parry** — *Read the hit and punish it.* Blocks the next incoming hit fully (**0 HP**) within a **3s** window; **1 charge**, spent by the hit it erases. **8 MP · 5s cd** (the cooldown deliberately exceeds the window, so parry can't be held up permanently).

- **As composed, Parry grants no damage bonus of its own.** The old "next-strike +50%" is not in the asset; the parry payoff now lives entirely in the **Opening** passive, so the two can't stack.
- ⚠️ **STALE — needs a design decision (2026-08-16).** Everything below was written when Retribution owned its own brace and Parry was its spine partner. **Brace is now a separate skill and Retribution's partner**, and the two do not interact: a parried hit is erased before any damage number exists, so it **banks nothing**. **Overclock** (was "Flurry") *(Blade Dance's spine partner)* — *Go faster, fragile.* **+30% Attack Speed for 8s, but take 2× damage for 3s.** The 2× drawback **flips per build**:

- **Blade Dance** (specialist): attack speed buys **more charges AND more parries** (both counts are derived from your swing rate), and it beats faster. ⚠️ **The old "its full invuln suppresses the 2×" no longer holds** — Blade Dance grants no invulnerability, so the 2× is a real cost you take with parries as the only cover. Reads as a bigger, more honest gamble than it used to.
- **Retribution:** 2× incoming → **2× bank** — drawback becomes upside.
- **Deathblow:** aspd → faster basics → Momentum quicker, but fragile for 3s.
- 12 MP · 12s cd. *(Watch-item: trends universally desirable; the 2× cost is the governor for every build now.)*

**Blade Dance** *(Overclock's specialist — BUILT 2026-08-16, and an **ULTIMATE** as composed — its full row is in the Ultimates table above)* — *Become the storm.* **40 MP · 45s cd · 11 range.** On cast it hands you **two piles that both last 8s and are both sized by your swing rate** — dance charges (`ceil(8 ÷ your seconds-per-swing)`) and **half that many, rounded up, in Parry charges**. Then it beats: **3 beats per swing**, each beat **spends one dance charge**, hops to the next of **7 star corners** leaving a **1s afterimage**, and strikes for **100% ATK**. Each beat re-aims at a random living mob within **3 units of the mob it just struck**, so the dance walks target to target; nothing in range ends it. **One beat, one target — this is single-target, never AoE.**

- Each held dance charge is also **+1% damage** (they are a stacking Damage buff), so the storm is strongest at its start and tapers as it spends down.
- **Defence is the Parry pile, not invulnerability** — it erases hits until it runs out, then you are exposed. Deliberate: a defence you can drain is a decision, not a freebie.
- **Measured consequence of the current tuning:** at base speed that is **14 slashes over \~2.8s** (14 × 0.2s) while the **parries last the full 8s** — so the guard outlives the dance by \~5s. Set beats-per-swing to 1 if you want the dance to fill its own window instead.
- *Engine note: no code names this skill. It composes from* `repeatCostsCharge` *+* `repeatScaleWithAttackSpeed` *+* `repeatReaim` *+ attack-speed-derived stack counts + the authored star corner count. See the implementation notes.*

**Lunge** — *Close the gap and strike.* Dash to a target + strike. The "reach" glue. 100% ATK · 8 MP · 5s cd · reach **8 world units** (`castRange`; the unit every range in this doc is written in — not navigation cells, which are 0.5 each). **Combo:** if you also rolled **Lock Blades**, casting Lunge grants it **5 charges** (tunable) — a Lunge+Lock Blades build keeps a near-constant supply of auto-lunges.

**Guard Stance** — *Weather it.* Take **50% less damage for 6s**. 12 MP · 9s cd. *(It was briefly authored backwards — a* `SubStatDebuff` *on* `DamageReduction`*, which the applier negates, so it INCREASED damage taken. Fixed to* `SubStatBuff` *on 2026-08-24 and verified off the asset. Overclock still uses the debuff kind deliberately — that is how it doubles incoming damage.)* ⚠️ **The asset's own description string still says "for 5 seconds" while the effect runs 6s** — one or the other should move. **Intentionally the weakest slot** (dominated by Parry/Blade Dance, anti-synergizes Retribution) — kept as the always-available sustain floor and a deliberate "not every roll is optimal" slot.

**~~Feint~~ — CUT 2026-08-18.** It read as Parry with damage bolted on: both were "read the incoming attack and answer it", and the roster only needs one such verb. Its payoff partner **Opening now rides Parry directly** (see passives), which also retires the mob-telegraph work Feint alone required. The slot it vacated is **open** — see the open-slot note below.

**Committed Strike** — *Wind up and commit.* A slow, telegraphed **heavy single hit** — the pool's one true **damage-typed** skill (Physical `damageMult`). **200% ATK · 16 MP · 8s cd · 1.2s cast · range 5.** Pairs with **Unwavering** (guard up so nothing shoves you out of the wind-up).

**Disarm** — **COMPOSED 2026-08-24.** *Blunt their swing.* A strike for **100% ATK** that hands the target **one charge that makes its next attack land 40% weaker**, held for 6s. **12 MP · 9s cd · range 5.** It weakens, it does **not** deny/stun — stays clear of Warhammer. Anti-burst tool when Retribution is on cooldown. *Engine note: the charge is the exact mirror of Opening's boost, spent by the attack it blunts whether that attack hits or misses.*

**Challenge** — **COMPOSED 2026-08-24.** *Make them fight you.* Taunts ONE enemy for **5s**: locked onto you, **cannot use skills**, and **no pause between swings** — its swing rate is untouched, the gaps are. **12 MP · 8s cd · range 11 · no damage** (the lock is the whole skill). Its point is Brace and Last Stand: a taunted target cannot decline to feed the bank. *Engine note: the taunt's rules now live on the taunt itself, so they hold for whoever it lands on — see the implementation items.*

**Offbeat** — **COMPOSED 2026-08-24.** *Break their rhythm.* A strike for **100% ATK** that cuts the target's **Attack Speed by 30% for 5s**. **12 MP · 8s cd · range 5.** Fewer swings means every Parry and Brace window covers more of the fight — timed defence bought from the offensive side. Not a Slow and not a stun: nothing is denied, only made rarer.

**Onslaught** — *Unleash a flurry.* An instant burst of **real basic attacks** (no skill `damageMult`; ATK-based) — so it feeds **Momentum** and rides every OnBasicAttack passive. **12 MP · 8s cd · range 11.** As composed it arms **5 stacks of +1% damage for 5s (ceiling 7)** and then spends them as the flurry's charges: **2 beats per swing**, one stack per beat, so the stacks ARE the shot count and the damage bonus tapers as it fires. Distinct from Overclock (a sustained *buff*); Onslaught is an on-demand Momentum dump.

**No active slots are open any more** — Challenge and Offbeat took the last two on 2026-08-24.

## Passive skills (9 composed)

Behavioral, event-triggered (via the trigger system Momentum proves). **Each ult has a special passive partner** — some universal-but-specialized, some ult-exclusive (fine: passives are rolled per build, so an exclusive one is build-defining, not dead). **Every one costs 0 MP and has no cooldown** — the pricing rule lives in **Skill Numbers & Balance Baseline**.

- **Momentum** *(Deathblow partner)* — see its own section above. `OnBasicAttackDealt` → **+1 stack, +3% damage each, 5s per stack, ceiling 99**; Deathblow consumes the pile.
- **Flow** *(Deathblow partner — replaces the old "Perfect Form")* — `OnBasicAttackDealt`, gated on the engine's **automatic run of hits** (`@run-of-hits`, the unit's unbroken run against ONE body): each basic attack grants **extra Momentum equal to ⌊run × 0.33⌋** — so about **+1 more per hit from the third hit of a run, +2 from the ninth**. The run is ended by the fight (a switched target, or a hit that dealt nothing), never spent by a skill. *Why the rename: the streak it needed is counted for every unit by the engine already, so the skill reads that instead of owning a second counter. Do not re-add Perfect Form.*
- **Encore** *(Blade Dance partner, exclusive)* — **BUILT.** `OnKill` **→ Blade Dance** grants **7 more dance charges**, genuinely lengthening the storm (the beat loop reads the LIVE pile each beat). It grants **no Parry charges**, so an extended dance runs past its guard — deliberate, and the reason a long dance is a gamble.
- **Last Stand** *(Retribution partner)* — **COMPOSED 2026-08-24, in a NEW shape.** `OnLowHP` → **arms 5 more Brace charges**. The old shape (the parry chain stops decaying) died with the chain itself; this one is a real partner again — the moment you are nearly dead is the moment you can hold more hits, and every held hit makes the Retribution release bigger. *Engine note: it adds stacks to Brace's own pile, so a charge is defined by Brace and never invented here; it works whether or not a Brace is already running.*
- **Composure** — **COMPOSED 2026-08-24, in a NEW shape.** `OnParry` → **MP returns at 2/s for 4s** (8 MP, about one reaction skill). The old shape — "your next skill costs 0 MP" — has no mechanism behind it and would need a whole free-cast system; this keeps the meaning (a good read pays for the next one) using the MP-regen sub-stat that already exists.
- **Unwavering** — `OnSkillCastComplete` **→ Guard Stance** grants **immunity to disables for 6s**, deliberately matching Guard Stance's own 6s (pairs with Committed Strike's long wind-up).
- **Opening** *(combos with Parry)* — **BUILT 2026-08-18, published.** **OnParry** → **a 50% chance** to grant **one charge of Boost Outgoing Damage at +200%, lasting 5s**. Your next attack is multiplied by it and spends it — **hit or miss**, because the charge pays for making the attack, not for landing it. The boost lands **before** the target's armour, so it is worth more the tankier the target. A combo passive (needs Parry rolled to fire): the read is the setup, the charge is the payoff. *(The 50% roll is what the asset holds and is the authority here — an earlier draft of this doc said the payoff was certain. Confirmed against the asset 2026-08-24.)*
  - *Engine note: no code names this skill. It composes from the* `OnParry` *trigger + a* `Boost Outgoing Damage` *effect. Parry itself grants only its own charges — its earlier press-time boost was removed so the two can't stack.*
- **Pressure** — `OnDamageDealt` → cuts that target's HP regeneration by **3/s for 5s**, and it **STACKS** (one stack per qualifying hit, ceiling 1000), so sustained pressure stops their healing outright. **Published 2026-08-24.** Note the trigger carries **no run-of-hits gate**, so it is *every* hit you land, not a consecutive same-target streak — an earlier draft of this doc said "consecutive"; the asset says otherwise. A regen rate at or below zero pays nothing, so this can never drain a target. *Its bite on mobs still depends on the mob-regen system (see impl items).*
- **Lock Blades** *(combos with Lunge)* — `OnSkillCastComplete` **→ Lunge** grants **5 charges** (tunable), each lasting **5s**: each hit you land **spends one charge to auto-lunge** the target again, so it can't disengage while you keep connecting. **Each held charge also grants +5% Attack Speed** (+25% at 5) — the charges are a stacking attack-speed buff, so a full pile both chases *and* swings faster, feeding Momentum while it lasts. **No Slow, no root** — the stickiness is on *you*, not a debuff on them. Out of charges, the passive does **nothing at all** (skill-level gate: no VFX, no lunge). Auto-lunges can't spend charges of their own — the no-self-feeding brake stops the chain. A combo passive (needs Lunge rolled to fire); leans **Deathblow** (denies disengage while you build/cash Momentum). *(Charges supersede the earlier duration-pin version, 2026-08-08 — see the **State System** doc, which owns the stacks/charges rules.)*

## Combos (setup → payoff)

- **Deathblow:** basic-attack to build Momentum, staying on ONE target so **Flow** keeps compounding the run → Deathblow (cash it). Overclock first to stack faster (fragile window).
- **Blade Dance:** Overclock (aspd → more charges, more parries, faster beat) → Blade Dance, **Encore** on every kill. The 2× damage taken is NOT suppressed any more; the Parry pile is your only cover.
- **Retribution:** Overclock (2× incoming) → **Brace** (the doubled hits bank at doubled value, 0 HP now) → Retribution to release and erase the debt. **Parry does NOT feed this** — a parried hit is erased before any damage number exists, so it banks nothing.

## Balance & implementation items (before/at build)

 1. **Attack Speed HARD CAP — DONE (2026-08-04).** `Character.AttackSpeedMult` (`Character.cs:370`) is now clamped by `MaxAttackSpeedMult = 3f` (`Character.cs:88`), whose comment cites Sword's Momentum as the reason. Momentum's equilibrium (attack rate × stack duration) is therefore bounded and Deathblow can't run away. Retune the `3f` value in playtest if the ceiling feels wrong.
 2. **Momentum tuning:** stack **duration** vs base attack interval so low-aspd settles \~3–4 stacks and capped-aspd lands at the target ceiling; **% attack per stack** (\~3% start).
 3. **Auto-parry telegraph VFX** (S6).
 4. **Retribution — DONE.** Brace banks the hit instead of costing HP; the release is uncapped and erases the debt. Its gate demands **at least one banked hit**, so with an empty bank the ult does not fire at all — no animation, no cooldown, no mana.
 5. **Mob attack telegraph lead-time — DROPPED for this roster (2026-08-18).** Measured (re-confirmed): a mob rolls and applies its bite in one moment, and its squash tell fires at that same instant — there is no wind-up. **Feint was the only skill that needed one, and Feint is cut**; Opening now rides Parry, which reacts to the hit itself rather than to a tell. Keep the finding on file: any future telegraph-reading verb (on any weapon) has to build the wind-up first, and it is a real system, not a tweak.
 6. **Healer mobs — CONTENT TODO.** Every unit now regenerates through the one shared ticker (see **Unified Unit Stats**), so **Pressure has something to cut** and is published. What is still missing is mobs that actively *heal* — the case where stopping regeneration is the difference between a kill and a stalemate.
 7. **Lock Blades ↔ Lunge combo — DONE (both assets composed).** Lock Blades is a passive triggered by `OnSkillCastComplete` parameterized to **Lunge**, granting **5 charges of +5% Attack Speed, 5s each**; Lunge carries the matching second trigger (`OnBasicAttackStart`, gated on that pile, spending one) so each landed hit auto-lunges. Cross-skill combo — only fires if both are rolled. Auto-pursue = the *caster* re-lunges; it is NOT a Slow/root on the target. Charge count still tunable.
 8. **Per-hit state/trigger order — LOCKED (from stress test).** One canonical order so combos resolve predictably every time: **(1)** hit lands → **(2)** **consume** one-shot states (Opening's charge, Disarm's weaken charge) → **(3)** apply damage/effects → **(4)** roll new procs → **(5)** apply newly-granted states. Consume-before-grant means a strike **can't self-consume** a charge it just created.
 9. **OnBasicAttack recursion guard (code TODO).** Onslaught and Momentum both key off basic attacks; a basic attack **generated** by another (Onslaught's flurry, Lock Blades' auto-pursue) must **not** recursively spawn more basics unless explicitly flagged — cap the chain at one level or the flurry feeds itself.
10. **Taunt now carries its own rules — DONE (2026-08-24), and they are engine-wide, not Challenge's.** A taunted unit (1) is locked onto its taunter, (2) **cannot start any skill it chooses to use**, and (3) **has no pause between swings** — a ranged mob loses its standoff and closes in instead of kiting. Its swing RATE is untouched: the taunt removes gaps, never shortens the interval. The window moved out of the two hand-rolled timers (one in the mob brain, one in the player rig) into the shared state store, so all three rules hold for whoever the taunt lands on, whether or not that unit has any skills at all. **Passives still fire** — a taunt removes the target's CHOICES, and a passive is not one. **Not covered: forcing another PLAYER to attack in PvP** — steering an AI and taking control from a player are different systems.
11. **Lock Blades "auto-pursue" — define ONE mechanism, engine-wide (code TODO).** "Your basic attacks automatically pursue" must resolve to a single behavior — **attack-lunge only** / **pathfind to target** / **forced facing** — chosen once so every future weapon reusing the primitive behaves identically. (Ties to #7.)
