Try: the tabs (the chosen one is filled), FIND, and Sort by. A tab holds 24 and shows all 24 boxes at once. Only All can need a scroll bar; it sits in its own lane outside the boxes, so nothing moves when it appears. Drag a box onto another to swap them, onto an empty box to move it to the end, or onto the hotbar below to put it there. While a sort is on, the order is the sort's, so dragging inside the bag does nothing (dragging to the hotbar still works). Hovering a box shows the game's own item tooltip, unchanged.
