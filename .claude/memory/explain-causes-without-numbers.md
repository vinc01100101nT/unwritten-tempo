When the user asks why something happens or how it works, answer in plain everyday words plus a simple picture.
Leave out measurements (metres, centimetres, counts, heights). Numbers still get MEASURED — they just are not the
explanation.

**Why:** 2026-09-30, explaining the cliff hole / solid grass sheet with "2.4 m", "3.9 m", "0.8 m grid": the user said
"do not give meters, explain in plain words. giving numbers does not help. give visual aid".

**How to apply:** for "why / what / explain" questions, describe the sequence of events in words and draw it (side
view, before/after, ✔/✘). Proof sections and bug reports may still carry numbers when the number IS the result.
Related: the RULE ONEGROUND in the project rules.
