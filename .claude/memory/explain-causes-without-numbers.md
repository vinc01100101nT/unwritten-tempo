When the user asks why something happens or how it works, answer in plain everyday words. Leave out measurements
(metres, centimetres, counts, heights). Numbers still get MEASURED — they just are not the explanation. Draw a
picture only when the user explicitly asks for one (the global RULE PICTURE, ruled 2026-10-02).

**Why:** 2026-09-30, explaining the cliff hole / solid grass sheet with "2.4 m", "3.9 m", "0.8 m grid": the user said
"do not give meters, explain in plain words. giving numbers does not help."

**How to apply:** for "why / what / explain" questions, describe the sequence of events in words. Proof sections and
bug reports may still carry numbers when the number IS the result. Related: the RULE ONEGROUND in the project rules.
