Every shuriken is held at its middle (the hub), never on a blade. A clearance fix for a shuriken may turn it, tilt
it or change its size; it may never move where the fist closes on it away from the middle.

**Why:** 2026-10-10, the 2x shuriken's points went into the head during its (locked) throw. In-plane turns could not
clear it, so I slid the hold out onto a blade (0% inside the body). The user: "dont change the handle of the
shuriken, hard rule for shurikens, all handles should always be at the middle, not on the blades. or else hands
will bleed". Reverted the same turn.

**How to apply:** before any shuriken hand-fit change, keep the fist on the hub; search for clearance among turns,
tilts and size only. Related: [[locked-attack-animations]] (the throw itself is locked).
