**THE MODEL (locked 2026-06-18):**
- **Draft = 1 of 4 each level: 3 known (a "Trio") + 1 random wildcard.** MVP skills are all REPEATABLE GENERIC STAT PASSIVES (no unique skills, no content).
- **The Ring:** the 10 stats sit in a fixed closed loop; a stat's Trio = itself + its 2 ring-neighbors. The Trio you see is a sliding window of width 3 centered on your current **leader** (your last pick). Pick center=stack (window stays); pick a side=that becomes new leader, window slides; pick wildcard=recenter on it. **Level 1 = all stats shown, no wildcard** (free starting point). The draft is a "steering wheel" — keep going / drift left / drift right / gamble.
- **Ring beats a line** because a line has degenerate endpoints; the ring closes the two damage poles (phys/magic) opposite each other with defensive/neutral stats bridging both arcs (can't walk phys→magic directly). **Ordering the ring IS the design lever** (pure tuning, no code). Candidate clockwise: Atk→AtkSpd→CritChance→CritDmg→Evasion→MAtk→MDef→MP→HP→Def→(back to Atk); one deliberate seam at Evasion↔MAtk.
- **Stats (10):** Atk, MAtk, HP, MP, Def, MDef, Evasion, AttackSpeed, CritChance, CritDamage (extensible).
- **Effect model = PERCENT multiplier layer, NOT flat.** Flat lives on level growth + stat-point distribution + equipment; passives are `Effective = flat × (1 + passive%)`. Why percent: never goes obsolete vs gear + level-independent relative value. **CORRECTION the user needed:** percent does NOT prevent runaway (additive % is still linear) — the "50% not 100%" feel needs a SEPARATE diminishing-returns curve.
- **Soft-cap DR (per stat):** `passive% = Cap × R/(R+K)` (R=ranks, Cap=ceiling, K=ranks to half-cap). Asymptotes to Cap. Bonus: marginal rank decays → math REWARDS diversification, reinforcing the ring's steering. Cap/K tunable (e.g. 80/40).
- **Cadence:** 1 point/level. Every level = passive draft. **Every 10th level = an ACTIVE-skill draft** (same 3+1 shape) — DESIGNED but POST-MVP (G2). MVP = passives only on every level.

**OUT of MVP:** actives/hotbar, persistence/save (none exists yet), respec item, seeded determinism (wildcard is live-random), balance pass, PvP.

**OPEN questions:** final ring order / seam; wildcard distribution (uniform-over-non-trio default vs distance-weighted); per-stat vs global Cap/K; draft UX (forced modal every level vs BANKABLE points spent in K panel — leaning bankable); wildcard re-roll (probably no).

Deltas vs the built §5/§6 model: **combine `CritChance+CritDmg`→Crit and `Def+MDef`→Defense (10→8 nodes)**, **add 2 new stats: Accuracy (anti-evasion pair) + CastSpeed (magic offensive momentum)**. Substat rules: crit ignores SOME def (30-50%, never 100%), crit & MAtk ignore Evasion (magic doesn't crit in unwritten — that's why the old Crit→MAtk seam is self-correcting). New risk list in §5b: Accuracy is secretly top-tier; CastSpeed must affect spells-only or AGI→magic seam opens; MP may be a tax stat; **forever-character homogenization is the #1 long-term unsolved (needs prestige/season/decay/cap-expand/spec-limit).** **Adopting §5b is a real refactor** (StatPanel/[[stat-distribution-system]]/code all built on the 10-stat split-crit/def set) — DECISION NEEDED before any code: 8-node matchup ring vs 10-node progression ring.
