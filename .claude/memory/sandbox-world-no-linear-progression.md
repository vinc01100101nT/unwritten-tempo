**The world is a sandbox, committed game-wide on 2026-09-26** (user: "no linear progression in the world").

- A creature's home biome, element, level and size are written on the creature; a map only lists which creatures live
  in it and never changes them. One creature can live from one end of the world to the other (like Porings).
- A map holds creatures of more than one level and element — residents near the level it is known for, plus weaker
  and stronger visitors — as long as each makes sense there. A map's level is a feel, never a gate.
- What still follows the CREATURE's level: its element tier (1–33 Neutral/Earth/Water; 34–66 +Wind/Fire; 67–99 all)
  and its drops (material grade bands).
- The mapping and its rules: the `world-authoring` skill (Creatures and Where They Live); the principle is
  in the `world-authoring` skill "The shape".

**Why:** the user wants Ragnarok-style spread, where creatures are not locked to one field.

**How to apply:** never propose level-gated regions, "zone for level X only" rules, or a creature that exists in one
map because of its level. Placing a creature = asking where it makes sense to live. See [[mobs-get-everything-players-get]].
