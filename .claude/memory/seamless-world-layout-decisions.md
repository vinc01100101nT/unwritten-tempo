Decisions for the one seamless world (handoff `handoffs/seamless-world.md`), made 2026-10-26/27:
- The continent is laid out FRESH — the old 90-map layout (`world-regions.json`) and the old continent draft are not the starting point.
- Only NATURE props are replaced by the Idyllic Fantasy Nature pack; buildings, houses and town structures stay ours.
- The lively pack look is the main look; a FEW dark biomes are kept as rare places (e.g. a swamp, a dungeon surround).
- Area borders are invisible, slightly wavy lines with NOTHING built along them (no river, no tree line, no wall); themes blend across them; props/paths run over them. Land is designed for itself, never shaped by borders.
- Rough layout first (land/sea, beaches, height, rivers+bridges, towns+roads, biome/theme regions, landmarks, dungeon doors) in ONE data file every area build reads, shown to the user as a top-down picture before anything is built; then area-by-area, each walked by the user before the next.

- Towns (2026-10-27): one weapon per town, 1:1. Brimstone is removed; the Sniper moves to Old Oak (open plains, walls, long sight lines). 15 towns: Lastline (start town, no weapon) + 14 weapon towns. Old Oak is placed on the Finesse-Arcane border in the new layout.
- Town themes follow each weapon's style (checked against the world design's style table, 2026-10-27): Lastline green trees + green willow, mixed meadows; Old Oak (Sniper, Dark Moon) open grass, lone oaks — no bright match; Parley (Sword, Blossom Wind) pink blossom + pink willow, pink meadows; Harrowen (Axe, Wildfire) green woods, orange + red-orange meadows; Pennaryl (Bow, Passing Cloud) thick forest + firs — no pack match; Gemmara (Warhammer, Earthrend) firs, rocks, cliffs; Tornel (Shuriken, Red Autumn) red trees + red willow; Velmora (Dagger, Nightshade Bloom) purple trees + purple willow, purple meadows; Hazemere (Katar, Veiled Mirage) DESERT (old props kept); Hilt (Greatsword, Raging Tide) BEACH near the Tide Temple; Pluvenne (Spear, Bending River) river: blue willows, reeds, lilies; Luminhal (Fist, Northern Lights) blue broadleaf, blue/blue-purple meadows; Halcyra (Wand, Morning Mist) blue willows, white meadows. SNOW DROPPED as a town theme (weather can snow anywhere).
- EVERY prop of the Idyllic Fantasy Nature pack must be used somewhere (user, 2026-10-27) — except its Water: river/pond/sea use the Genshin water. Demo tools (Controller, WindControl, VegetationBendControl) are not props. Themes were reshuffled so every tree colour and meadow colour has a home; enforce it with a check that fails the world build when a pack prop is unused.
- Plant WIND uses the pack's own values, NOT scaled to the world (2026-10-27): gusts ~0.67 units wide, travel 1.4 units/s, strength 0.25. Scaling it by 0.172 made gusts a third of a person wide and moved plants ~4 px at the game camera — the user lost the rolling waves they liked. Only the player BEND is scaled.
- Layout preview decisions (2026-10-27): dark places on their own dark isle off the south-west coast, by boat — Kharnel, Cindrel, Drowned Chapel, Masked Ballroom, Dusk Tower, Everburning Plain, Burning Keel; the continent stays bright. Hilt is the BEACH town (sand, rocks, cliffs; beach plants need another pack). The layout is WRITTEN (2026-10-27): data/world-layout.json, made only by tools/world-layout/build_world_layout.py (places decided by hand in its PLACES block; coast, height, rivers, themes, roads worked out; same output every run). Its map goes to ~/Downloads/world-layout.html (a "Show build squares" toggle draws the 32-unit chunk grid). Ponds: by Lastline, by Pluvenne, in Pennaryl's forest, and a desert oasis for Hazemere — each placed on flat walkable ground inside its own town's theme.
- Getting there (2026-10-27): Luminhal and the dark isle by BOAT, far enough out to sea that a boat makes sense; the Tide Temple on its own islet beside the coast, reached on foot by a low-tide path (the design's "walkable only while the tide is out"). Coasts are freehand noise shapes (seed 7).
- Cliffs and caves (2026-10-27): the land is cut into plateau levels (five steps of 3–5 units); MOST of each level edge is a sheer cliff ("cliffs everywhere", user 2026-10-27), the rest a walkable slope, so no area is shut in; any remaining fall-in pit gets a ramp cut; rivers run in wide valleys (not walled gorges, which made pits). The build refuses to write a world with a fall-in pit nobody can walk out of. Halcyra is HIGH GROUND (about 29 units), sea cliffs on its shore, one long walkable slope down to Tornel (about 17). Raised mesas with cliffs all round are scenery (can be neither entered nor fallen into). Four caves like the test map's: under the northern mountains (Gemmara), a sea cave in Hilt's headland cliffs, one in a forest ridge west of Pennaryl, one on the dark isle near Kharnel.

**Why:** user hates square-border looks ("rivers everywhere… square rivers and treelines"); the book's world is "peace and lively".
**How to apply:** never propose borders made of features; never swap buildings for pack models; towns keep their buildings; only their nature surroundings take the theme.
