---
name: feedback-no-redundant-options
description: When proposing a list of options/variants, no two may serve the same purpose (mana leech vs mana regen = one job)
metadata:
  type: feedback
---

Every option I put in a proposed list (variants, words, perks, stats) must do a DIFFERENT job. Two options that serve the same purpose are redundant padding, even if their mechanics differ.

**Why:** 2026-09-23 armour words — I offered both Siphoning (mana leech) and Wellspring (mana regen), and life steal beside HP regen. The user: "stop giving redundant context like mp steal and mp regen, they both serve the same". They also wanted the variety WIDER, not the same idea twice.

**How to apply:** before sending a list, group items by the player-facing purpose (health back, mana back, damage up, damage down, mobility, control resistance…). One item per purpose; spend freed slots on new purposes. Relates to [[feedback-proceed-is-not-go-while-questions-open]].
