Every number on every skill in the game — Sword and all other weapons, plus skills not yet attached to any weapon:
damage, defence, damage cuts, durations, costs, everything — is a placeholder, not yet tuned for balance (user,
2026-10-18).

**Why:** the skills were authored to make mechanics work, before any balance pass.

**How to apply:** never treat a skill's number as a design decision to preserve or balance other systems against;
when a budget (e.g. the 6-point skill share of hard defence, see [[defence-split-and-gear-budget]]) needs a skill
changed, just retune it — no question needed about "breaking" its current value.
