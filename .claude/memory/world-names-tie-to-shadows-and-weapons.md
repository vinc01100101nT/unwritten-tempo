**Each shadow is a Kashira with a name and a title, and fights in a STYLE** (the names, titles and styles are LOCKED
since 2026-10-06; the table is in `docs/3-world/story-setting-canon.md`). A shadow shows its name and title and is also
called by its style. Every skill of a weapon is a numbered FORM of its style (learning order), and a form's name uses
only its own style's imagery (Blossom Wind → wind, petals, branches, storm). The skill's name IS the form name, and a
trademark weapon is called by it. Inspired by Demon Slayer's breathing styles.

**The 14 styles, all in one POETIC register** — a picture of nature (a living thing, a natural scene, or the weather or
season that moves it), never a material or machine: Passing Cloud (Bow), Bending River (Spear), Raging Tide
(Greatsword), Earthrend Descent (Warhammer), Northern Lights (Fist), Wildfire (Axe), Blossom Wind (Sword), Morning Mist
(Wand), Autumn Gale (Shuriken), Nightshade Bloom (Dagger), Frozen Ashes (Staff), Black Lily (Scythe), Dark Moon
(Sniper), Veiled Mirage (Katar). The register was chosen because a single soft style (Blossom Wind) among hard ones
(Iron Gate, Bedrock…) felt wrong. Do not reopen them.

**What the user accepts in a style name** (learned over ~8 rounds of rejections on 2026-09-26):
- It must sound smooth said with "style" — "Ash style", "Endless Sky style", "Tide style" were rejected as awkward.
- A real English word or phrase, spelled as English spells it. Joined-up coinages confused them ("Blackwater" reads
  as two words; "Fogbreaker" became "Fog Breaker").
- Not always two-part: one word or three words are welcome — a mix, not a pattern.
- **Never another franchise's term**: no "Breathing", "Beast" (Demon Slayer), "Windrunner" (Warcraft), "Halo",
  "Harvest Moon". Check before proposing.

**Place names (2026-09-26):** about ONE THIRD real English (from one syllable like "Hilt" to several words like "Hill of
the Flame") and the rest MADE-UP names that sound like Ragnarok locations (Prontera, Payon, Geffen, Aldebaran) — the user
asked for this mix after an all-English list. A made-up NAME is fine; a made-up LANGUAGE (sound rules, word order) was
rejected on 2026-09-25 and stays rejected, as does any real country's culture. Each place still ties to its shadow's
style, its weapon and its setting.

**Locked:** every town's name and the weapon it belongs to (the theme list), and every shadow's place — both in the `world-authoring` skill, section 2. Do not reopen them unasked.

**How to apply:** for each name, say in one line how it links to the deed, the element and the place; offer a pick
plus alternatives; check the sound with "style" and the franchise risk before offering. See
[[the-five-anti-invention]].
