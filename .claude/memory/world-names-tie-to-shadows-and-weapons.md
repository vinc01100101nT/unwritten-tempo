When naming regions, towns or places in the world design, ALWAYS tie the name to the homeland's shadow (Varro,
Brigid, Radomir, Kallias, Brandr, Inés, Morwenna, Fiora, Azar, Ulrich, Temülün, Tsubame) and to its weapon.
Style: Ragnarok city names (Prontera, Payon, Morroc, Al De Baran, Geffen, Umbala, Lutie) — 1 to 4 syllables,
varied shapes, including some one-off formats like "Al De Baran" (several words, still ≤4 syllables).

**Why:** the user rejected two rounds of names — first all same-pattern compounds, then names with only a culture
link and no tie to the shadows/weapons. The twelve homelands exist BECAUSE of the twelve shadows.

**How to apply:** for each name, be able to say in one line how it links to its shadow and its weapon; vary
syllable count and format across the set. See [[the-five-anti-invention]] — names are still proposals.
