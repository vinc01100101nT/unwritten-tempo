The user said on 2026-10-13: "all the service, items being sold, all their prices are not yet final, we determine their rates after we figure out the player income first. npc items and services come after."

**Why:** I had justified an income curve (10 gold x creature level) by saying it kept today's forge, fold, quench and shop costs meaningful. Those costs are placeholders, so they are not a constraint.

**How to apply:** when planning gold income (creature material sale values, saved-character gold, quest rewards), never argue from a current price or cost. Settle income first; then derive forge, fold, quench, shop, keeper and other service prices from it. Same-day direction: no raw gold drop — income is selling each creature's own material to an NPC, value following the creature's level.
